MODE is the digital operations and institutional knowledge platform supporting the Ormoc City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) and Emergency Operations Center (EOC).

### Supporting statement

**From institutional guidance to coordinated action. From operational learning to continuous improvement.**

### Section: Why MODE matters

Disaster risk reduction is not delivered by a platform alone. It depends on capable institutions, clear responsibilities, trusted information, practical procedures, coordinated partnerships and sustained learning.

MODE is designed to support these foundations by making operational guidance easier to access and use, connecting institutional assessment with improvement actions, and helping preserve the records and learning that strengthen the City's DRRM and EOC systems.

### Section: What MODE brings together

**The Manual of Operations**  
A structured digital companion to the City's CDRRMO and EOC Manuals, with access to controlled content, procedures and related guidance. The approved manuals remain the authoritative documents.

**Institutional assessment and improvement**  
A structured way to assess institutional capacities, record supporting evidence, identify gaps and organise follow-up actions. Scores and maturity results must be read with their cycle, evidence and validation status.

**Operational workflows and protocols**  
Step-based guidance and supporting forms designed to help authorised personnel carry out defined procedures consistently. Actual authority remains with the officials and offices designated by law and City issuances.

**EOC coordination and records**  
Digital support for operational logs, situation reporting, resource coordination and related EOC records, subject to user roles and approved operating procedures.

**Risk information and decision support**  
Access to mapped and documented risk information, with source, date and validation context. These materials inform analysis; they are not forecasts or official warnings.

**Field-oriented and offline-capable access**  
A mobile-first experience designed to support access in connectivity-constrained settings. Users must check sync status and follow established fallback procedures when information cannot be synchronised.

### Section: How the platform supports improvement

1. **Assess** — document institutional capacities, gaps and supporting evidence.
2. **Review and validate** — engage the responsible offices and technical stakeholders to examine findings and proposed procedures.
3. **Operationalise** — make approved guidance and workflows accessible to authorised personnel.
4. **Learn and improve** — use implementation records, exercises and after-action learning to inform future revisions.

### Section: Built around institutional roles

MODE supports the City's DRRM governance and operating arrangements. It does not reassign statutory mandates or replace the CDRRMC, CDRRMO, EOC, Incident Commander, Incident Management Team or other authorised offices and officials.

### Section: Participate in strengthening the Manual

A useful Manual must be technically sound, practical in the field, understood by the offices responsible for implementation and consistent with approved City policies and applicable law.

CDRRMO, City departments, barangay representatives and partner stakeholders have an important role in reviewing and validating the procedures relevant to their mandates.

## 2. About (`/about`)

**Eyebrow:** ABOUT THE PLATFORM

# A digital companion for Ormoc City's DRRM and EOC operations

MODE supports the development, use and continuous improvement of the Ormoc City CDRRMO Manual of Operations and EOC Manual. It is intended to make institutional guidance more accessible, connect assessment with practical improvement, and support the documentation of operational processes and learning.

## Why MODE was developed

Effective disaster risk reduction and emergency operations require more than plans and equipment. They rely on institutions that understand their roles, personnel who can find and apply procedures, information that can be assessed in context, and coordination arrangements that work across offices and communities.

MODE provides a shared digital environment for parts of this institutional work. It helps organise the Manual and its supporting procedures, capture assessment and validation evidence, guide defined workflows, and maintain operational records for authorised users.

## The platform supports the Manual — it is not the Manual

The City's duly approved CDRRMO Manual of Operations and EOC Manual remain the authoritative documents. MODE provides a digital means of accessing and supporting their use. A screen, workflow, score, map or database entry does not amend an approved Manual, City policy or legal mandate.

Any proposed change to controlled Manual content must follow the City's established review, validation and approval arrangements. Digital publication must not be treated as approval unless the authorised process has been completed and recorded.

## Contributing to a resilient Ormoc

Ormoc City's development and resilience aspirations provide the wider context for strengthening local DRRM institutions. The City's Comprehensive Development Plan expresses its development vision, while the City's Ten-Year Local DRRM Plan sets a direction for advancing disaster resilience. MODE is one enabling instrument within this broader institutional effort; it is not a substitute for the plans, investments, partnerships and public action needed to achieve those aspirations.

**Publication note:** Insert the exact, approved wording and document references for the City vision and Ten-Year LDRRM Plan only after checking the controlled source documents, their adoption status and the applicable plan period. Do not paraphrase an official vision inside quotation marks.

## Institutional roles and boundaries

- **CDRRMC:** provides the policy direction, coordination and oversight assigned under applicable law and local issuances.
- **CDRRMO:** serves as the City's permanent DRRM office and provides the technical and institutional functions assigned to it.
- **EOC:** serves as an activated coordination mechanism under the City's approved arrangements.
- **Incident Commander and IMT:** retain their established incident command and management functions under the applicable ICS arrangements.
- **MODE:** provides digital access, workflow support, assessment, information management and records within approved user permissions. It does not create or transfer statutory authority.

## Who uses MODE

Access is intended for authorised City personnel and designated stakeholders whose responsibilities require use of the platform. Accounts, roles and permissions are assigned through the City's approved administration process. Access to information is limited according to the permissions configured for the user.

## Design principles

- **Institutional ownership:** the platform supports City processes and should remain aligned with approved City policies and operating arrangements.
- **Practical usability:** guidance should be understandable and usable by personnel working across office, EOC and field settings.
- **Evidence and traceability:** assessment, validation, operational records and Manual revisions should retain appropriate source and status information.
- **Interoperability of roles:** the platform should support coordination without blurring mandates or command relationships.
- **Continuous improvement:** exercises, operational experience and after-action learning should inform controlled improvements.
- **Responsible technology:** offline use, privacy, access control, accessibility and data quality must be treated as operational requirements.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (`/faq`)

### About MODE

**What is MODE?**  
MODE is the digital operations and institutional knowledge platform supporting Ormoc City's CDRRMO and EOC Manuals. It brings together Manual content, procedures, institutional assessment, risk information and operational records for authorised users.

**Does MODE replace the approved Manuals?**  
No. The approved CDRRMO Manual of Operations and EOC Manual remain the authoritative documents. MODE is their digital operational companion. A change in the platform does not, by itself, amend or approve the official Manuals.

**Does MODE make Ormoc City resilient?**  
No platform can achieve resilience on its own. MODE is intended to support institutional capabilities, access to procedures, coordination, evidence and learning. Resilience depends on the City's wider governance, investments, services, partnerships and community action.

### Authority and emergency operations

**Does MODE issue official hazard warnings or advisories?**  
No. Follow official information and advisories from PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, MGB and other agencies with the relevant mandate. MODE may help authorised users organise and access risk information, but it is not an official warning service.

**Does MODE issue evacuation orders or declare a State of Calamity or State of Imminent Disaster?**  
No. These decisions and declarations remain with the authorities designated by law. MODE may support documentation and coordination of the applicable process; it does not make the decision or confer authority.

**Does MODE replace the Incident Commander or the Incident Management Team?**  
No. The EOC supports coordination under the City's arrangements. Field command and incident management remain with the designated Incident Commander and IMT under the applicable ICS structure.

**Can an EOC activation be treated as a declaration of a State of Calamity?**  
No. EOC activation and a declaration under disaster-related law are distinct processes. Follow the applicable law, approved City procedures and authorised decision-makers.

### Accounts and access

**Who can use MODE?**  
Users must have an account approved through the City's administration process and a role appropriate to their responsibilities.

**Why can I not see a record or module?**  
Access depends on the permissions assigned to your account and the status of the relevant record. Contact the designated MODE administrator or CDRRMO focal person if you believe access is incorrect.

**Can I share my account?**  
No. Use your own account, protect your sign-in credentials and report suspected account compromise or a lost device through the approved support channel.

### Offline use and synchronisation

**Can MODE work offline?**  
Some information and functions may be available offline, depending on what has been made available on the device and the function being used. New entries may remain on the device until connectivity returns. Check the displayed sync status and do not assume that an unsynchronised entry has reached the City's central system.

**Can every action be completed offline?**  
No. Actions that require current authoritative information, approval, coordination or other controlled server-side checks may require a live connection. Follow the on-screen status and the City's approved fallback procedures.

**What should I do if a record is waiting to sync or shows a conflict?**  
Keep the device available, avoid repeatedly submitting the same action, review the sync status and follow the instructions shown in the application. If the issue remains, contact support and provide the page, approximate time, device and browser. Do not email passwords or sensitive information about affected persons.

### Assessment, evidence and Manual validation

**What does an institutional assessment score mean?**  
A score represents the assessment result for a defined indicator and assessment cycle. It must be interpreted with its scoring criteria, evidence, validation status and applicable denominator. A score is not, by itself, proof of legal compliance or operational readiness.

**Does uploading evidence mean that an indicator is validated?**  
No. Evidence submission and evidence validation are distinct. Follow the assigned review and validation process and check the status shown for the record.

**Can stakeholders edit or approve the Manual directly?**  
Only users with the relevant permissions may contribute to the workflow. Review comments, technical recommendations and validation inputs do not automatically constitute formal approval. Approval remains with the official or body authorised under the City's process.

### Risk information

**Are the maps and risk profiles forecasts?**  
No. They are decision-support materials based on identified sources and available data. Check the source, date, geographic coverage, limitations and validation status before using them. They do not replace official warnings or technical assessments.

### Privacy and support

**What should I avoid entering into MODE?**  
Follow the City's data-protection and records-management instructions. Do not enter unnecessary personal or sensitive information about affected people into free-text fields or send such information through ordinary email.

**How do I report a technical problem?**  
Use the official support contact shown on the Support page. Include the affected page or module, approximate time, device/browser and a short description. Never send passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary personal data.

**Is the Support page an emergency channel?**  
No. The website and support email are not a substitute for the City's emergency hotline, EOC communications or official emergency reporting channels. In an emergency, use the established City and barangay procedures and follow official advisories.
