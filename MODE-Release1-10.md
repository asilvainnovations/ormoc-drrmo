# MODE 1.10.0: what changed (8 Nov 2026)

- **Definitions.** SOP = controlled Manual text. Protocol = point-of-action checklist (daily operations, warning, EOC, anticipatory action, reporting, recovery). Workflow = multi-office management cycle. Form = the record that closes a step. These are in the Annex 3-N glossary and ADR-019.
- **SOP registry** (migration `20261108002000_sop_registry.sql`). All 23 templates now carry kind, controlling SOP, process owner, policy references and IAT indicators:
  - 8 protocols and 15 workflows;
  - 161 steps and 40 forms;
  - the view `v_indicator_sops`.
- **Chapter 3 v1.0** (working draft). The source limitations were resolved from the CDRA, CLUP and DRRM 10-Year Plan. The barangay count is 85, with a 110→85 crosswalk. The chapter has SOP control blocks (Tables 3.3–3.11), APA figures 3.1–3.20 and the Annex 3-N glossary. In the platform it runs as workflows WF-SOP3-01..09 with Forms 3-01..3-11. Still missing: the CDRA's adopted Chapter 5–6 tables and the OCD contingency-planning manual.
- **Public site** (ADR-020):
  - Routes: `/`, `/about`, `/faq`, `/support` and `/policies/{privacy,terms,accessibility,cookies}`. The workspace is at `/today`.
  - Policies are corrected to the code: the City is the controller, hosting is in Singapore, no cookies are set, and there are no AI features. They show "Draft, not yet approved" until `VITE_POLICY_EFFECTIVE` and `VITE_POLICY_OWNER` are set.
  - robots.txt and sitemap.xml are generated at build. The workspace sends noindex.
- **Also in this release:** SECURITY.md, the CI workflow, a Library search, IAT workbook v1-6 (reference data unchanged), and the removal of the unused shadcn ui kit (140 TypeScript errors).
- **Tests:**
  - 203 unit tests, the 260 SQL checks and 9 integration tests pass.
  - The e2e suite passes 91 tests per device. The 3 sign-up and reset tests fail because the test gateway lacks email endpoints.
