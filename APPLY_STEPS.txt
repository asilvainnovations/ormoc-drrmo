E. ACCEPTANCE / SMOKE TESTS
- Active authorised user can open /governance.
- Review Register, Decisions, Workshop and Stakeholders pages load from Dexie after sync.
- Search/filter behaviour works; empty states are useful when seed is absent.
- An active user without an eligible governance role cannot update governance records through direct API calls.
- Eligible roles can update non-authority fields.
- Review closure fails without disposition and closure test.
- Formal decision fails without disposition and decision reference.
- Only the permitted authority roles can close/reopen/record decisions as defined by the database guards.
- Offline reads and permitted draft capture behave consistently; formal authority actions require a live connection.
- Repeated seed does not duplicate rows.
- RLS and trigger behaviour is verified for every relevant role.
- No 85-barangay final denominator is introduced; OD-08 remains unresolved pending City masterlist reconciliation.
- Stakeholder matrix remains proposed reference data and does not grant MODE application roles.
