# ANNEX 2-K \| SOP HR-05 – DUTY ROSTER, SHIFT HANDOVER AND PERSONNEL AVAILABILITY

**Purpose**: Maintain reliable personnel coverage and safe transfer of responsibility during normal operations and heightened readiness.

**Owner**: CDRRMO Head; division leads prepare and supervise rosters.

**Procedure**:

1\. Determine required coverage from approved service hours, hazard season, duty requirements and continuity plan.

2\. Confirm personnel availability, qualifications, alternates, leave, rest periods and constraints.

3\. Prepare roster and submit for approval under delegated authority.

4\. Publish roster securely and notify assigned personnel.

5\. Outgoing officers brief incoming officer on open tasks, warnings, incidents, contacts, equipment status, pending approvals and risks.

6\. Both officers acknowledge handover; record time, unresolved issues and escalation.

7\. Immediately report absence, unfilled post, unsafe fatigue or inability to cover a critical function to the supervisor.

8\. Arrange authorized replacement and record changes.

9\. Reconcile actual coverage, attendance and exceptions monthly; feed gaps to CAPA.

Records: duty roster; acknowledgement; attendance; handover checklist; exception and fatigue log.

Boundary: this SOP does not define EOC activation level, incident command or operational period; refer to the designated EOC/ICS chapters.
