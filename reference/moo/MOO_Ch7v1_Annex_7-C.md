# Annex 7-C. After-Action Review Report Outline

**Table 7.41**

*Contents of an AAR Report (ME-F03)*

| **No.** | **Section**                 | **Content**                                                                                |
|---------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1       | Event and scope             | Event or exercise, dates, hazard, area, EOC level, declarations; what the AAR covers       |
| 2       | Method and participants     | Facilitator; offices and positions taking part; sources used                               |
| 3       | Timeline                    | Official warning products with IDs; decisions; activations; key SitReps; resource requests |
| 4       | What was supposed to happen | Plans, SOPs and protocols that applied                                                     |
| 5       | What happened               | Facts, with evidence                                                                       |
| 6       | What worked                 | Practices to keep, with evidence                                                           |
| 7       | What did not work and why   | Findings with root or contributing causes; single- or double-loop                          |
| 8       | Actions                     | Recommended actions with owner, due date and resources, entered in the CAPA register       |
| 9       | Inclusion and protection    | How women, children, older persons and persons with disabilities were reached; gaps        |
| 10      | Approval                    | CDRRMO Head approval; items referred to the CDRRMC                                         |

*Note.* Records name positions, not individuals. Personal data of affected people is not included.
