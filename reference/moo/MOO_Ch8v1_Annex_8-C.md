# Annex 8-C. Model Outline of a Part C Interface Agreement

**Table 8.35**

*Outline of an Interface Agreement Between the CDRRMO and a Lead Office*

| **No.** | **Section**           | **Content**                                                                                                        |
|---------|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1       | Parties and approval  | CDRRMO and the lead office; approved by the City Administrator (or included in a Mayor's EO)                       |
| 2       | Indicators owned      | Part C indicator codes and statements from IAT v1.7                                                                |
| 3       | Focal persons         | Positions and alternates on both sides                                                                             |
| 4       | Actions for the year  | From the IAT recommended actions and the CAPA register; outputs and dates                                          |
| 5       | Information exchanged | What the CDRRMO supplies (hazard maps, scenarios, event data) and what the lead office supplies (evidence, status) |
| 6       | Role in events        | PDRA participation; anticipatory actions; reporting to the EOC                                                     |
| 7       | Review                | Annual interface review; self-scoring with validation by another office                                            |
