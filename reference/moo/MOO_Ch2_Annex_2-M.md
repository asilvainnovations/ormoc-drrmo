# ANNEX 2-M \| SOP HR-07 – PERSONNEL SAFETY, WELFARE, INJURY AND PSYCHOSOCIAL REFERRAL

**Purpose**: Prevent harm and ensure prompt support for CDRRMO personnel and responders under the City's responsibility.

**Owner**: CDRRMO Head for implementation; HRMO, designated OSH personnel and health/MHPSS providers for their respective professional mandates.

**Procedure**:

1\. Identify task hazards and required controls before assignment; verify PPE, training, fitness and supervision.

2\. Brief personnel on hazards, stop-work authority, communications, emergency contacts and welfare arrangements.

3\. Monitor exposure, fatigue, hydration, rest, heat, psychosocial stress and changing conditions.

4\. If injury, exposure, unsafe condition or distress occurs, prioritize immediate safety, medical care and protective action.

5\. Notify supervisor and designated safety focal; complete incident report as soon as practicable without delaying care.

6\. Refer to appropriate medical, occupational health, insurance, HR or psychosocial service with consent and confidentiality safeguards.

7\. Assess return-to-duty, accommodation and restrictions through competent professionals and HRMO processes.

8\. Review causes and contributing system factors; implement corrective actions and verify effectiveness.

Records: task risk assessment; PPE issue register; briefing; incident/near-miss report; referral record with restricted access; corrective action and return-to-duty clearance.

Safeguards: protect sensitive personal information; do not disclose diagnosis or counselling details in operational logs; comply with applicable OSH, privacy and mental health requirements.
