# ANNEX 3-N \| Glossary of Terms Used in This Chapter

Terms follow RA 10121, the DHSUD CDRA guidance and the definitions used in Chapters 1 and 2. Where an official definition exists, it governs.

**Table 3.12**

*Glossary of Terms*

| **Term**                                    | **Meaning in this Manual**                                                                                                                                  |
|---------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Adaptive capacity                           | The ability of people, institutions and systems to adjust to, prepare for and recover from hazard and climate impacts.                                      |
| BIA (business impact analysis)              | Assessment of how the disruption of an essential function affects people and the City over time; the basis for recovery priorities (Form 3-07).             |
| CDRA (Climate and Disaster Risk Assessment) | The DHSUD-prescribed assessment that links hazard and climate information to exposure, vulnerability and risk, and to land-use decisions.                   |
| Contingency plan                            | An approved, hazard-specific plan setting scenario, triggers, roles, resources and reporting before an event (SOP 3-6).                                     |
| Critical point facility / lifeline          | A facility (hospital, school, EOC) or network (road, power, water, telecommunications) whose failure affects many people or services.                       |
| Exposure                                    | People, buildings, production areas, facilities and lifelines located where a hazard may occur.                                                             |
| Failure chain                               | A traced sequence from a hazard through an asset failure to a service disruption and its consequences (SOP 3-4).                                            |
| Form                                        | The record a step is closed on. In MODE every workflow and protocol step has a form; the Manual's form where one exists, otherwise the one-tap step record. |
| MDA (Major Decision Area)                   | A place or issue where CDRA results require a land-use, investment, social-protection or ecosystem decision (Form 3-04).                                    |
| Modelled potential damage                   | The value of exposed assets that could be damaged under a model's assumptions; not an observed loss or an annual expected loss.                             |
| Process owner                               | The office accountable for an SOP producing its result; others are responsible, consulted or informed (Section 18).                                         |
| Protocol                                    | A step-by-step operational checklist run at the point of action (for example warning dissemination); it records who closed each step and when.              |
| PSCP (Public Service Continuity Plan)       | The City plan that keeps essential functions running during disruption (SOP 3-7).                                                                           |
| Risk Information Register                   | The controlled list of risk datasets with source, custodian, date, scale, access class and version (SOP 3-2).                                               |
| SOP (standard operating procedure)          | The approved, controlled text in the Manual that states what is done, by whom, under what authority and with what records.                                  |
| Validation status                           | How far information has been checked: reported, preliminary, field-verified or official (Geo-COP layers; IAT evidence).                                     |
| Workflow                                    | An SOP made executable in MODE: a sequence of steps across offices over days or weeks, each closed on its form.                                             |
