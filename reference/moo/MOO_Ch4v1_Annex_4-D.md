# ANNEX 4-D \| GAWAD KALASAG CY 2026 EVIDENCE CROSSWALK

The Gawad KALASAG LDRRMO assessment toolkit (CY 2026) asks for evidence that Chapter 4 records already produce. Keeping the records as the procedures require produces that evidence as a by-product.

**Table 4.45**

*Chapter 4 Records Used as Gawad KALASAG Means of Verification*

| **Toolkit criterion (CY 2026)**                | **Means of verification asked for**                                                                                                         | **Chapter 4 record or procedure**                                      | **Where it is kept**                                           |
|------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Established local DRRM operations center (1.8) | EO or similar issuance creating a 24/7 OpCen; OpCen duty logbook; identified alternate EOC                                                  | SOP HR-05 handover (HR-F04); SOP 4-01 log; alternate EOC under SOP 3-7 | MODE: logbook, DRRM protocols; Vault: the EO                   |
| Early warning system (A.2, 2.3)                | SOP or manual on EWS covering risk knowledge, monitoring and warning, dissemination and communication, response capability                  | SOP 4-01, 4-02; matrix register (Annex 4-C); EWR-F01, EWR-F03          | Library: Chapter 4; MODE: protocol runs                        |
| Response under alert conditions                | Order or memorandum raising and downgrading the alert status; PDRA attendance and minutes; deployment memorandum; ICS forms; timely reports | SOP 4-03, 4-04, 4-05, 4-08, 4-09; EOC-F01, EWR-F04, EOC-F03, EOC-F05   | MODE: EOC level history, SitReps, protocol runs; Vault: orders |
| Contingency plans                              | LDRRMC-approved, Sanggunian-adopted hazard-specific plans for at least the top two hazards                                                  | Chapter 3 SOP 3-6; activation in SOP 4-04                              | MODE: EOC → Plans and clusters; Vault                          |
| ICS in place                                   | Updated IMT organisational structure; IMT members trained; AAR of a SIMEX based on the SOP or CP                                            | SOP 4-05; SOP 4-09 AAR (ER-F06)                                        | MODE: roster, protocol runs; Vault: AARs                       |

*Note.* Criteria and means of verification paraphrased from the OCD Gawad KALASAG LDRRMO assessment toolkit, CY 2026 (checklists S, C and MS). The toolkit governs; this crosswalk only shows where the evidence is kept.
