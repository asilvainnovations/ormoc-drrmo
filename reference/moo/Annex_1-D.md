## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE GOV-04: STATE OF CALAMITY DECLARATION, IMPLEMENTATION AND LIFTING

Purpose. To establish a documented procedure consistent with Section 16 of RA 10121 and its IRR.

Critical authority rule. The local sanggunian declares or lifts a State of Calamity upon recommendation of the LDRRMC, based on damage assessment and needs analysis. A Mayor's Executive Order shall not substitute for the Sanggunian's statutory declaration.

| **Control**        | **Requirement**                                                                                                                               |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Process owner      | CDRRMO Head                                                                                                                                   |
| Recommendation     | CDRRMC, based on validated assessment                                                                                                         |
| Decision authority | Sangguniang Panlungsod, as provided by law                                                                                                    |
| Core evidence      | RDANA; affected population; casualties; infrastructure / livelihood damage; service interruption; urgent needs; response capacity             |
| Records            | Assessment, Council minutes / recommendation, Sanggunian resolution, implementation and resource records, lifting recommendation / resolution |

### Procedure

Receive and log reports from BDRRMCs, response agencies, utilities and City offices; record source, time, location and verification status.

1\. Receive and log reports from BDRRMCs, response agencies, utilities and City offices; record source, time, location and verification status.

2\. Conduct and consolidate RDANA using approved methodology and competent sector offices; distinguish verified facts, estimates and information gaps.

3\. Prepare a technical assessment describing scale, severity, affected sectors, urgent needs, available capacity and basis for or against recommending declaration.

4\. Submit assessment to CDRRMC for deliberation and recommendation where statutory criteria are met.

5\. Transmit complete recommendation and evidence package to the Sangguniang Panlungsod for action through the legally prescribed process.

6\. Upon valid Sanggunian resolution, implement authorized remedial measures, assistance and reporting.

7\. Maintain post-declaration action register and monitor impacts, service restoration, resources and outstanding needs.

8\. When warranted, recommend lifting; the Sanggunian shall act through the applicable legal process.

9\. Archive the decision trail and conduct an after-action review.

Required records: incident report; RDANA; CDRRMC attendance, minutes and recommendation; Sanggunian resolution; action and resource register; public information; lifting recommendation and resolution. CLO shall verify statutory criteria, current IRR, resolution form, publication/posting and lifting process.
