## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE GOV-04: STATE OF CALAMITY DECLARATION, IMPLEMENTATION AND LIFTING
**SOP-DRRM-GOV-04 · Draft for legal validation and adoption by the appropriate local authority**

## 1. Purpose
This SOP establishes a consistent, evidence-based and auditable process for recommending, declaring, implementing and lifting a State of Calamity in Ormoc City.

It is intended to ensure that the decision is based on documented damage assessment, needs analysis and applicable legal criteria, and that the resulting interventions are coordinated, time-bound, properly funded and subject to monitoring.

## 2. Legal Basis
The principal legal basis is Section 16 of Republic Act No. 10121 and its applicable IRR, together with relevant provisions of the Local Government Code and applicable national and local issuances.

**Statutory decision rule**: The Sangguniang Panlungsod declares and lifts a State of Calamity upon the recommendation of the LDRRMC, based on the assessment of damage and needs. The SOP must not be interpreted as authorizing the City Mayor, CDRRMO or EOC to independently exercise the sanggunian's statutory declaration or lifting authority.

## 3. Scope
This SOP applies to:

* The CDRRMC;
* CDRRMO and EOC;
* City Government departments and offices;
* Barangays;
* Concerned national government agencies;
* Local response organizations; and
* Other institutions requested to support assessment, response and recovery.

## 4. Guiding Principles
1. **Legality**: All recommendations and actions shall comply with applicable law.
2. **Evidence**: Decisions shall be supported by verified damage, needs and impact information.
3. **Proportionality**: Recommended measures shall be appropriate to the scale, severity and duration of the emergency.
4. **Inclusiveness**: Assessments shall identify the differentiated needs of women, children, older persons, persons with disabilities, Indigenous Peoples where applicable, low-income households and other at-risk groups.
5. **Accountability**: Decisions, resource commitments and actions shall be recorded and traceable.
6. **Coordination**: All offices shall operate through agreed reporting and coordination arrangements.
7. **Recovery orientation:** Declaration shall support timely response and recovery, not substitute for longer-term risk reduction and reconstruction planning.

## 5. Triggers for Assessment and Consideration
A State of Calamity may be considered when a disaster has occurred and the available evidence indicates that the applicable statutory criteria have been met.

Potential triggers include:

* Significant loss of life or injury;
* Extensive damage to homes, public infrastructure, livelihoods or essential services;
* Large-scale displacement or evacuation;
* Disruption of critical lifelines or basic services;
* Emergency needs exceeding the capacity of affected communities or local response arrangements; and
* Other conditions prescribed by applicable law and regulations.

These are operational screening considerations, not substitutes for the exact statutory criteria. The City Legal Office shall verify the legal threshold and documentary requirements before adoption.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities
| Actor | Primary responsibility |
| ----- | ----- |
| Barangay DRRM Committees | Initial incident reporting, local validation, affected-population information and urgent needs reporting |
| CDRRMO | Coordinates assessment process, consolidates evidence and prepares technical recommendation |
| EOC | Maintains incident log, COP, situation reports and resource status |
| City Engineering / Building Official | Assesses damage to buildings, public facilities and infrastructure within their mandates |
| City Health Office | Assesses health impacts, medical needs and public health risks |
| City Social Welfare and Development Office | Validates displacement, household needs and social protection requirements |
| City Agriculture / Veterinary Office | Assesses damage to crops, livestock, fisheries and agricultural livelihoods |
| CPDO | Supports spatial analysis, development impact analysis and recovery planning |
| Other technical offices | Assess impacts within their statutory and technical mandates |
| CDRRMC | Reviews evidence and adopts the recommendation within its mandate |
| City Mayor | Convenes and provides executive coordination; transmits the recommendation to the sanggunian |
| Sangguniang Panlungsod | Exercises the statutory authority to declare or lift the State of Calamity |

## 4.7 Procedure
### Step 1. Incident reporting and initial validation
The barangay or concerned office shall report the incident to the CDRRMO or EOC through the established reporting channel.

The EOC shall record:

* Date and time of incident;
* Location and affected barangays;
* Hazard type and available official warning information;
* Initial reported casualties and affected households;
* Initial damage and service disruption;
* Immediate needs; and
* Source and verification status of the information.

The EOC shall distinguish between initial reports and validated findings.

### Step 2. Activation of assessment arrangements
The City DRRM Officer shall recommend the activation of the appropriate assessment teams or technical working groups.

The CDRRMO shall coordinate with relevant offices to define:

* Assessment objectives;
* Geographic coverage;
* Sectoral responsibilities;
* Data collection methods;
* Reporting timelines;
* Required technical expertise; and
* Consolidation arrangements.

The assessment shall use applicable national tools and methodologies, including relevant damage assessment, needs analysis and post-disaster assessment protocols.

### Step 3. Conduct of damage and needs assessment
Each responsible office shall assess impacts within its mandate.

The assessment shall, as applicable, document:

1. Human impacts, including deaths, injuries, missing persons and displacement;
2. Housing damage and habitability;
3. Damage to public buildings and infrastructure;
4. Disruption of water, power, telecommunications, transport and other lifelines;
5. Health, sanitation and protection needs;
6. Agriculture, fisheries, livestock and livelihood losses;
7. Education and social service disruption;
8. Environmental impacts and access constraints;
9. Priority needs of vulnerable sectors; and
10. Estimated response and early recovery requirements.

Each report shall identify the assessment date, geographic coverage, methodology, responsible assessor, source records and level of confidence.

### Step 4. Consolidation and technical analysis
The CDRRMO shall consolidate the sectoral reports and prepare a citywide assessment summary.

The EOC shall support consolidation by maintaining:

* Incident chronology;
* Affected-area map;
* COP;
* Resource and assistance tracker;
* Situation reports; and
* Outstanding information requests.

The consolidated report shall identify information gaps and distinguish confirmed figures from estimates.

### Step 5. CDRRMC deliberation and recommendation
The CDRRMC shall review the consolidated assessment and determine whether the evidence supports recommending the declaration of a State of Calamity.

The deliberation shall consider:

* Applicable statutory criteria;
* Scale and severity of impacts;
* Geographic extent;
* Capacity of affected barangays and City Government;
* Urgency and nature of required interventions;
* Expected duration of disruption;
* Resource and financing implications; and
* Risks of further deterioration.

The Council's recommendation shall be documented in a resolution or other legally appropriate instrument, supported by the assessment report.

### Step 6. Submission to the Sangguniang Panlungsod
The CDRRMC Secretariat shall transmit the recommendation, assessment report and supporting documents to the City Mayor and Sangguniang Panlungsod through the appropriate official channels.

The submission shall include:

* CDRRMC resolution or recommendation;
* Consolidated damage and needs assessment;
* Maps and sectoral reports;
* Summary of affected population and facilities;
* Proposed geographic coverage;
* Recommended scope and duration of emergency measures; and
* Other documents required by law or the sanggunian.

### Step 7. Sangguniang Panlungsod action
The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall deliberate and act on the recommendation in accordance with applicable law and its rules of procedure.

If approved, the declaration shall be embodied in the appropriate sanggunian resolution or other legally required instrument.

The resolution shall clearly state:

* The legal basis;
* The affected geographic areas;
* The factual basis and assessment references;
* The date of effectivity;
* The authorized measures and relevant limitations; and
* Any reporting or review requirements.

### Step 8. Dissemination and implementation
Upon declaration, the CDRRMO and EOC shall coordinate dissemination to:

* All City Government offices;
* Barangays;
* Concerned national agencies;
* Utilities and service providers;
* CSOs and private sector partners; and
* Affected communities through appropriate communication channels.

Each responsible office shall implement measures within its legal mandate and approved authority.

The declaration shall not be treated as an automatic exemption from procurement, accounting, auditing, environmental, building safety, data protection or other applicable requirements.

### Step 9. Monitoring and reporting
The CDRRMO shall maintain a consolidated monitoring record covering:

* Response and early recovery actions;
* Resource mobilization and utilization;
* Assistance delivered;
* Unmet needs;
* Service restoration;
* Population movement and displacement;
* Emerging risks; and
* Progress toward recovery.

The EOC shall maintain operational logs and situation reports for the duration of activation.

### Step 10. Review and recommendation for lifting
The CDRRMO shall periodically review whether the conditions that justified the declaration continue to exist.

The review shall consider:

* Restoration of essential services;
* Reduction of immediate life-safety threats;
* Evacuation and displacement status;
* Access to affected communities;
* Remaining urgent humanitarian needs;
* Status of critical infrastructure;
* Capacity of local systems to manage residual impacts; and
* Transition from emergency response to recovery arrangements.

When conditions warrant, the CDRRMC shall deliberate and recommend lifting the State of Calamity to the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

### Step 11. Lifting by the Sangguniang Panlungsod
The Sangguniang Panlungsod shall exercise the statutory authority to lift the declaration, upon recommendation of the CDRRMC and in accordance with applicable law.

The lifting instrument shall specify the geographic scope, effective date and any continuing administrative or recovery arrangements.

Lifting the declaration shall not automatically terminate ongoing recovery, rehabilitation, social protection or reconstruction programs.

### Step 12. Post-incident review and records closure
Within the period prescribed by the MOO or applicable policy, the CDRRMO shall convene an After-Action Review with the participating offices and partners.

The review shall identify:

* What worked;
* What did not work;
* Delays and coordination failures;
* Resource and capability gaps;
* Data and communication issues;
* Good practices;
* Corrective actions; and
* Responsible offices and completion dates.

The Secretariat shall archive the declaration, assessment, resolutions, reports, operational logs and post-incident review.

## 4.9 Mandatory Records
The following records shall be maintained:

* Initial Incident Report;
* EOC Incident Log;
* Assessment Activation Order or Memorandum;
* Sectoral Assessment Reports;
* Consolidated Damage and Needs Assessment;
* Maps and affected-area information;
* CDRRMC meeting minutes and resolution;
* Submission to the Sangguniang Panlungsod;
* Declaration or non-approval record;
* Resource and assistance tracker;
* Situation reports;
* Review and lifting recommendation;
* Lifting instrument; and
* After-Action Review and Improvement Plan.
