# ANNEX 4-B \| EOC AND INCIDENT COORDINATION RACI WITH LEGAL DECISION AUTHORITY

Operational accountability (A) and legal decision authority are different things. The second column names who decides in law or under a City issuance; the other columns repeat the proposed RACI of section 15.

**Table 4.42**

*Legal Decision Authority and Operational Accountability*

| **Activity**                             | **Legal or formal decision authority**                                                    | **Process-accountable (A)**           | **Responsible (R)**                                        |
|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| Warning receipt and verification         | None (no decision)                                                                        | CDRRMO Head                           | Operations and Warning (OpCen)                             |
| Warning-action recommendation            | Official or body named in the approved CP or EOC EO                                       | CDRRMO Head (technical process)       | Operations and Warning                                     |
| Evacuation and public protective action  | Official or body named by law and City issuance                                           | Competent authority                   | Barangays (implementation); CDRRMO (recommendation)        |
| Raising or lowering the EOC alert status | Official designated in the EOC EO; LGU by EO on LDRRMC recommendation (DILG VIII s. IV.3) | Designated authority                  | EOC Manager (implementation); CDRRMO Head (recommendation) |
| EOC coordination and reporting           | Mayor/CDRRMC for policy decisions                                                         | CDRRMO Head (institutional oversight) | EOC Manager and sections                                   |
| Field incident command                   | Incident Commander under the ICS EO                                                       | Incident Commander / IMT              | Agencies within their command                              |
| Resource requests with expenditure       | Authorised officials under Chapter 5                                                      | CDRRMO Head                           | EOC sections; asset owners                                 |
| SitRep submission                        | Approving official in the reporting route                                                 | CDRRMO Head (oversight)               | EOC Planning / situation unit                              |
| Deactivation and transition              | Official designated in the EOC EO, on the IC recommendation                               | Designated authority                  | EOC Manager; sector offices                                |
| State of Imminent Disaster               | Mayor by EO after the RA 12287 route (SOP GOV-05)                                         | CDRRMO Head as CDRRMC secretariat     | See Chapter 1                                              |
