# ANNEX 2-L \| SOP HR-06 – EOC PERSONNEL DESIGNATION AND SURGE AUGMENTATION

**Purpose**: Ensure personnel assigned to EOC functions are designated, available, competent and supported by their parent offices.

**Owner**: CDRRMO Head for roster readiness; EOC Manager for activated EOC staffing coordination; HRMO for personnel administration.

**Procedure**:

1\. Identify EOC functions and minimum competencies from the approved EOC structure and job action sheets in the relevant chapters.

2\. Request nominations from City offices and partner agencies, including primary and alternate personnel.

3\. Verify role fit, training, contactability, availability, supervisor concurrence and conflict/continuity implications.

4\. Compile a controlled designation register; obtain approval by the authority specified in the EOC issuance.

5\. Conduct orientation and exercises; record gaps and refresher needs.

6\. On activation, EOC Manager requests staffing through approved activation procedures; parent offices release or substitute personnel consistent with authority and service continuity.

7\. Track attendance, shift rotation, welfare, access permissions and return-to-office arrangements.

8\. Deactivate or revise assignments through authorized procedures; reconcile records and lessons.

Records: designation instrument; roster; alternates; competency evidence; exercise records; release/return log.

Control: designation to an EOC position does not transfer the person's statutory agency mandate or create field command authority.
