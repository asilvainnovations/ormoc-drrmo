# ANNEX 2-E. DUTY HANDOVER CHECKLIST

- Current hazard bulletins, warnings and validity periods.

- Active incidents, affected locations and current operational status.

- Pending decisions, approvals, requests and deadlines.

- Resource requests, deployment status and unresolved constraints.

- Communications, ICT, facility and equipment status.

- Personnel availability, welfare concerns and relief arrangements.

- Records, logs, reports and attachments completed or pending.

- Escalation items and person / office responsible for follow-up.
