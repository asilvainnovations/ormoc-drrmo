# Annex 8-B. Model Outline of a Technical Mutual Aid Agreement

**Table 8.34**

*Outline of a Technical Mutual Aid Agreement*

| **No.** | **Article**                     | **Content to be negotiated**                                                                                   |
|---------|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1       | Parties and authority           | LGUs; sanggunian resolutions authorising each Mayor; optional accession by the Province                        |
| 2       | Purpose and principles          | Technical cooperation in DRRM; reciprocity; no transfer of authority; each party first protects its own people |
| 3       | Definitions                     | Requesting party, assisting party, resources, deployment, host Incident Commander                              |
| 4       | Resources covered               | Types of teams, equipment and services; annexed lists updated yearly                                           |
| 5       | Activation                      | Who may request; request content; response time; right to decline                                              |
| 6       | Command, control and safety     | Under the host IC (ICS); own supervisors; right of recall                                                      |
| 7       | Logistics                       | Self-sufficiency period; food, lodging and fuel arrangements                                                   |
| 8       | Costs and settlement            | What each party bears; reimbursement method; timing                                                            |
| 9       | Liability and insurance         | Personnel and equipment insurance; claims; indemnity                                                           |
| 10      | Information and data            | Directory, warnings and SitRep exchange; data protection                                                       |
| 11      | Exercises and review            | Annual joint exercise or call-down; annual review                                                              |
| 12      | Term, amendment and termination | Duration; renewal; notice period                                                                               |
