# Annex 5-B. Recovery RACI With Legal Decision Authority

**Table 5.41**

*Legal Decision Authority Kept Apart From Operational Accountability*

| **Decision**                                            | **Legal or approving authority**                          | **Operational accountable owner (A)**          |
|---------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **Adoption of the pre-disaster recovery framework**     | CDRRMC                                                    | CDRRMO Head                                    |
| **Conduct of a City PDNA**                              | CDRRMC                                                    | CDRRMO Head                                    |
| **Approval of the PDNA**                                | CDRRMC (RDRRMC VIII review when NDRRMF funding is sought) | PDNA coordinator                               |
| **Approval of the rehabilitation and recovery program** | Sangguniang Panlungsod, on CDRRMC endorsement             | CPDO (technical lead)                          |
| **Appropriations and supplemental budgets**             | Sangguniang Panlungsod                                    | City Budget Officer                            |
| **Building permits and structural acceptance**          | Building Official                                         | Proponent office                               |
| **Land use and zoning changes**                         | Sangguniang Panlungsod (ordinance)                        | CPDO / Zoning Administrator                    |
| **Relocation and resettlement**                         | Authorities under RA 7279 and DHSUD rules                 | Housing office with CSWDO                      |
| **Eligibility for social services**                     | Each service office under its rules                       | CSWDO, CHO, PESO, City Agriculture as relevant |
| **Procurement awards**                                  | Head of procuring entity on BAC recommendation            | BAC secretariat (Chapter 6)                    |

*Note.* Authorities follow the NEDA (2020) guide and the general legal framework; the City Legal Office shall confirm each row before adoption.
