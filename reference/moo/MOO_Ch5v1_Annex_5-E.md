# Annex 5-E. Minimum Data for the Recovery Baseline

**Table 5.43**

*Minimum Recovery Baseline Data, Disaggregation and Source*

| **Element**                          | **Data and disaggregation**                                                                        | **Likely source**                  |
|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **General information**              | Land area; topography; barangay boundaries (PSGC)                                                  | CPDO; CLUP; NAMRIA; PSA            |
| **Income and poverty**               | Income class; poverty incidence by barangay                                                        | BLGF; PSA; CBMS                    |
| **Population**                       | Total population by barangay, urban/rural, sex, age group and disability                           | PSA; CBMS                          |
| **Settlements and housing**          | Settlement areas and population; housing materials; informal settlers; land use and zoning map     | CLUP; Shelter Plan; CDRA           |
| **Economic activity and livelihood** | Labour force; MSMEs; farmers and fisherfolk; agricultural areas and facilities; informal sector    | PESO; BPLO; City Agriculture; CDRA |
| **Infrastructure and lifelines**     | Roads and bridges; power, water and telecom facilities; port; public buildings; evacuation centres | City Engineering; utilities; CDRA  |
| **Social services**                  | Schools; health facilities; social welfare facilities and caseloads (aggregates)                   | DepEd; CHO; CSWDO                  |
| **Environment**                      | Protected areas; watersheds; forest and mangrove cover                                             | CENRO; DENR; LCCAP                 |
| **Hazards and past events**          | Hazard maps; past damage and losses by event                                                       | CDRA; RDANA and PDNA reports       |

*Note.* Adapted from Annex A of the NEDA (2020) guide. Household-level data stays with the source office; the baseline holds aggregates by barangay.
