## EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. \_\_\_\_ SERIES OF 20\_\_\_\_

## RECONSTITUTING THE ORMOC CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT COUNCIL, UPDATING ITS MEMBERSHIP AND ALTERNATES, AND PRESCRIBING ITS GOVERNANCE AND SECRETARIAT ARRANGEMENTS

WHEREAS, RA 10121 and its IRR provide for local DRRM councils and their functions; WHEREAS, the City requires updated representation, regular deliberation and documented follow-through; NOW, THEREFORE, I, \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, City Mayor of Ormoc City, hereby order:

## Section 1. Reconstitution.

The CDRRMC is reconstituted in accordance with Section 11 of RA 10121 and its IRR. The final membership shall be listed in Schedule A, completed and legally checked before signature.

## Section 2. Membership and alternates.

Each member institution shall designate a principal and alternate in writing. CDRRMO shall verify all statutory seats, including required accredited CSO representation and private-sector representation, against the Act and IRR.

## Section 3. Functions.

The Council shall exercise statutory policy, coordination, recommendation, plan review, resource oversight and monitoring functions.

## Section 4. Meetings.

The Council shall meet at least once every three months and as necessary, consistent with applicable rules.

## Section 5. Secretariat.

The CDRRMO shall serve as Secretariat and maintain meeting notices, agendas, technical papers, attendance, minutes, resolutions, decision register and action-monitoring reports.

## Section 6. Decision authority.

Council recommendations shall be distinguished from decisions reserved by law to the Mayor, Sanggunian or another competent authority.

## Section 7. Working groups and inclusion.

Technical working groups may be established with terms of reference. CSO, private-sector, barangay and sector participation shall be meaningful and consistent with statutory composition.

## Section 8. Effectivity.

This Order shall take effect upon signature, subject to applicable law.
