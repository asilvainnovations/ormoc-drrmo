Consistent definitions. CDRRMC means City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council; CDRRMO means permanent City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office; EOC means Emergency Operations Center as an operational coordination mechanism; ICS means Incident Command System; IMT means Incident Management Team; PDRA means Pre-Disaster Risk Assessment; RDANA means Rapid Damage Assessment and Needs Analysis. These definitions are descriptive and do not confer authority beyond applicable law.

Document hierarchy. Philippine statutes and valid implementing rules prevail over administrative issuances, local executive issuances, approved plans, SOPs and forms. RACI designations allocate work but do not create statutory powers or transfer mandates.
