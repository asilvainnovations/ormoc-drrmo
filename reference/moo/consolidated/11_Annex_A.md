# Annex A. Master Procedure Register

This register lists every procedure in the Manual in one place. It is the process inventory for quality management: one line per process, one owner, one quality stage and one key record. The quality stage shows the main role of the procedure in the PDCA cycle; it is assigned for management review and does not change the SOP.
