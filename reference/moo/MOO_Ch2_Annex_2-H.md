# ANNEX 2-H \| SOP HR-02 – POSITION DESCRIPTION AND COMPETENCY PROFILE CONTROL

**Purpose**: Keep every authorized position's duties, outputs, authority boundaries and competency requirements current and aligned with statutory CDRRMO functions.

**Owner**: HRMO for official position-description control; CDRRMO Head for technical content.

**Procedure**:

1\. Identify new, revised or materially changed function.

2\. Division lead drafts role purpose, outputs, duties, reporting line, decision limits, interfaces, working conditions and competency profile.

3\. CDRRMO Head verifies alignment with RA 10121 and approved functional architecture.

4\. HRMO checks consistency with qualification standards, position classification and CSC requirements.

5\. CLO reviews legal implications where authority or delegation is affected.

6\. Obtain required approval; assign version and effective date.

7\. Communicate role and performance expectations to incumbent and supervisor.

8\. Review annually and after organizational, legal or operational change.

Records: approved PDF, competency profile, consultation record, approval and revision history.

Control: a job description cannot expand statutory authority or substitute for a valid delegation.
