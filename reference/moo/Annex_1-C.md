## EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. \_\_\_\_ SERIES OF 20\_\_\_\_

## ESTABLISHING AND OPERATIONALIZING THE ORMOC CITY EMERGENCY OPERATIONS CENTER, DEFINING ITS FUNCTIONS, ACTIVATION AND DEACTIVATION, AND CLARIFYING ITS INTERFACE WITH THE CDRRMC, CDRRMO AND INCIDENT COMMAND SYSTEM

WHEREAS, RA 10121 requires effective local DRRM coordination; WHEREAS, the City requires a defined operational coordination mechanism; WHEREAS, EOC coordination must not displace CDRRMC/CDRRMO mandates or ICS incident command; NOW, THEREFORE, I, \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_, City Mayor, hereby order:

## Section 1. Establishment.

The City EOC is established as an operational coordination mechanism administered by the CDRRMO and activated under approved triggers and procedures.

## Section 2. Functions.

The EOC maintains situational awareness; validates and disseminates incident information; supports executive and CDRRMC decision-making; coordinates resource requests; facilitates inter-office and inter-agency coordination; maintains incident and decision records; and supports transition to recovery.

## Section 3. Non-command status.

The EOC is not a field command structure. The Incident Commander retains incident-level command under applicable ICS arrangements. EOC coordination does not alter agency mandates or operational control.

## Section 4. Management.

The CDRRMO Head oversees readiness. A designated EOC Manager manages activated operations under approved job action sheets and reporting lines.

## Section 5. Activation.

Activation may be triggered by official warnings, PDRA findings, actual or imminent impacts, multi-barangay incidents, critical service disruption, resource coordination needs or direction of the Mayor / authorized official. The MOO shall specify levels and authority.

## Section 6. Deactivation.

The authorized official shall deactivate the EOC when coordination needs have sufficiently reduced and transition arrangements are established. Closure includes logs, resource reconciliation and after-action review.

## Section 7. Readiness and continuity.

The CDRRMO shall maintain duty rosters, contact directories, handover procedures, communications redundancy, facility checklists and minimum staffing arrangements, subject to approved resources.

## Section 8. Information and records.

The EOC shall maintain incident logs, situation reports, decision logs, resource records and dashboards subject to privacy, security, records and disclosure requirements.

## Section 9. Resources and effectivity.

Implementation is subject to approved staffing, appropriations and procurement rules. This Order takes effect upon signature.
