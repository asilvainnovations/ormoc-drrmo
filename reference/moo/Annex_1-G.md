# ANNEX 1-G | GOVERNANCE FORMS, TEMPLATES AND REGISTERS

*City Government of Ormoc | CDRRMO–EOC Manual of Operations*

**PROPOSED – FOR LEGAL / TECHNICAL REVIEW**

## Document Status and Use

This Annex contains proposed controlled forms to support governance, decision-making, accountability, documentation and institutional learning under the Ormoc City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (CDRRMC) and Emergency Operations Center (EOC) arrangements. It is a drafting aid and is not, by itself, an authorization to exercise a statutory power or approve expenditure.

Before adoption, each form and its workflow must be checked against the latest certified statutes and implementing rules, current national and regional guidance, the City's approved organizational structure, plantilla, delegations, budget and accounting rules, records disposition schedule, privacy policies, and applicable City procedures. Legal review is particularly important for forms dealing with declarations, executive action, regional requests, lifting, and the exercise of emergency powers.

## Standard Rules for All Controlled Forms

Every form shall display City Government of Ormoc, form code, version, date, page number, preparer, reviewer, approving authority where applicable, classification, records custodian and related SOP / Chapter reference.

Use the form code as the unique identifier. Do not change a controlled form's fields or approval route without document-owner review and version control.

Record dates and times using a consistent format (YYYY-MM-DD; 24-hour time with Philippine Standard Time where relevant). Identify the source and timestamp of material information.

Distinguish verified facts, estimates, forecasts, assumptions and recommendations. Identify the office or source responsible for each material entry.

Attach evidence using a unique attachment number and maintain a traceable link to the official record or EOC incident log.

Collect personal information only when necessary for a defined lawful purpose. Apply data minimization, access controls, secure transmission, retention and disposal safeguards under RA 10173 and applicable rules.

Do not include sensitive personal data in broadly circulated council minutes or situation reports unless strictly necessary and lawfully authorized. Use a restricted annex or case reference where appropriate.

Use “N/A” for non-applicable fields; explain material omissions. Corrections to signed records must preserve the audit trail.

The preparer, reviewer and approving authority must be identified by position and office; signatures confirm the action actually performed and do not replace any statutory approval.

## Completion guidance / safeguards

Prioritize certified official sources: Official Gazette, official government agency issuances, authenticated local ordinances / resolutions and verified City records.

Record the exact instrument number, date, issuing authority and status. Do not rely solely on summaries, unofficial reposts or superseded copies.

The responsible owner should assess operational implications and identify affected chapters, SOPs, forms, training and delegations.

Conduct periodic verification and trigger an immediate review when a new law, rule, advisory, ordinance or court ruling may affect operations.
