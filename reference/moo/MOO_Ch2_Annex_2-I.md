# ANNEX 2-I \| SOP HR-03 – RECRUITMENT, APPOINTMENT INTERFACE AND ONBOARDING

**Purpose**: Ensure staffing actions are based on authorized positions and comply with civil-service and City HR rules.

**Owner**: HRMO; CDRRMO provides technical role requirements and induction.

**Procedure**:

1\. HRMO confirms an authorized vacant plantilla item and applicable qualification standards.

2\. CDRRMO validates technical competencies and operational requirements without adding unauthorized eligibility criteria.

3\. HRMO processes publication, assessment, selection and documentation under the approved Merit Selection Plan and applicable CSC rules.

4\. Selection/recommendation is documented; appointing authority exercises appointment power.

5\. HRMO completes appointment, assumption and personnel-record requirements.

6\. CDRRMO supervisor conducts induction on mandate, MOO, role boundaries, safety, information security, privacy, records and coordination protocols.

7\. Agree initial outputs, supervision and learning needs; record completion.

8\. Review onboarding effectiveness and update induction materials.

Records: vacancy certification; approved PDF/QS; recruitment records; appointment; assumption; induction checklist; initial performance plan.

Control: CDRRMO may not promise appointment, bypass HRMO or deploy an appointee before lawful assumption and required clearances.
