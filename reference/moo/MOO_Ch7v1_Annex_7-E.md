# Annex 7-E. Disaster Event and Loss Database Fields

**Table 7.43**

*Minimum Fields of the Event and Loss Record (ME-F07)*

| **Field group**                      | **Fields**                                                                                                                                     | **Sendai link** |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Event                                | Event ID; name; hazard type; start and end dates; source agency product IDs; declarations in force                                             | —               |
| Location                             | Barangays affected (PSGC codes); area description                                                                                              | —               |
| Human effects                        | Dead; missing; injured; affected persons and families; displaced in and outside evacuation centres (aggregate, by sex and age where available) | A, B            |
| Housing                              | Houses partially and totally damaged                                                                                                           | B, C            |
| Economic loss                        | Direct loss by sector (agriculture, infrastructure, housing, business), PHP, with valuation method                                             | C               |
| Critical infrastructure and services | Damaged health and education facilities, roads, bridges, power and water; duration of service disruption                                       | D               |
| Response and AA                      | Whether an EOC activation, State of Calamity or State of Imminent Disaster applied; AA carried out                                             | G               |
| Sources and quality                  | Source document for each figure; date; quality flag; reviewer                                                                                  | —               |

*Note.* Aggregate counts only. Blank means "not reported". Small counts are reviewed by the DPO before publication.
