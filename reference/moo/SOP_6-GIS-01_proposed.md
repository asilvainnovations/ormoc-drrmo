# SOP 6-GIS-01 | Establishment, Updating and Operational Use of the EOC Geospatial Common Operating Picture

**Status:** proposed SOP for the EOC Operations, Information Management, Logistics and Coordination chapter (MOO Chapter 6, not yet drafted). Loaded as a controlled draft so the Geo-COP workflow (WF-GEO-COP) points to an existing text. Source: the 6 November 2026 note "Proposed Enhancement to the EOC Module: Geospatial Operations and Common Operating Picture", section 3, unchanged except for this header.

Title: Establishment, Updating and Operational Use of the EOC Geospatial Common Operating Picture

Purpose: To ensure that the EOC maintains a consistent, validated and operationally useful geographic representation of incidents, affected areas, facilities, resources and response operations.

| Step                          | Responsible actor                                      | Required action                                                                                                                 | Output / control                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 1. Establish reference        | EOC Information Management function / CDRRMO           | Register the EOC point from the supplied KML and verify the coordinates through field validation.                               | EOC geospatial reference register         |
| 2. Acquire datasets           | EOC Information Management function                    | Obtain available base maps, hazard layers, administrative boundaries and critical-facility datasets from authorized custodians. | Geospatial data inventory                 |
| 3. Validate data              | Designated data custodian / technical office           | Check source, date, coordinate system, geometry, completeness and consistency.                                                  | Data validation record                    |
| 4. Integrate layers           | EOC Information Management function                    | Load approved datasets into the designated GIS or mapping platform and apply agreed layer naming and symbology.                 | Geo-COP operational map                   |
| 5. Plot incident information  | EOC Situation Unit or designated information personnel | Record incident locations and spatially reference reports received from barangays, field teams and agencies.                    | Georeferenced incident records            |
| 6. Verify field information   | Concerned technical office / field team                | Confirm material incident locations, access constraints and reported impacts through appropriate field verification.            | Verification status and supporting report |
| 7. Update operational picture | EOC Information Management function                    | Update incident, evacuation, resource and infrastructure layers as validated information becomes available.                     | Time-stamped Geo-COP                      |
| 8. Disseminate                | EOC Manager / authorized information officer           | Share approved map products with decision-makers and operational partners according to access permissions.                      | Situation map / map briefing              |
| 9. Archive and review         | EOC Information Management function                    | Preserve operational map versions and underlying records for incident review, reporting and after-action analysis.              | Archived map versions and metadata        |

Operational control: Each map product shall indicate its preparation date and time, data sources, verification status and intended use. Preliminary or unverified information must be visibly distinguished from confirmed information.

The SOP should be activated as part of the EOC's information-management function whenever the EOC is activated. During normal operations, the CDRRMO shall maintain the underlying datasets, registers and readiness arrangements.

**Related records in MODE:** forms EOC-GIS-REF and EOC-GIS-01 to EOC-GIS-04; EOC → Map (ADR-015). Chapter 4 (SOP 4-05) uses the Geo-COP for the incident situation; Chapter 6 owns this procedure when drafted.
