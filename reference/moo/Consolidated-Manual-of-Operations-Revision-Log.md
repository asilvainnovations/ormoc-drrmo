## 2. Cross-references repaired
- Ch.1 section 3: description of Introduction section 3 corrected.
- Ch.6 (two places): GPPB Res. 17-2026 / Circular 08-2026 no longer "listed as in force in the Introduction, section 12 (Table I.16)"; now "cited in project planning notes; not yet verified". Certified copy still required from the BAC Secretariat.

- Introduction section 10.6: named individual removed (role retained).

## 3. Structure and readability
- Executive Summary now has its own Heading 1; its sections and the Introduction's sections are demoted one level, so chapters are the only Heading 1s after the Introduction.
- Ten empty or stray heading paragraphs un-styled (one was a full sentence styled as Heading 3).
- Captions unified to the two-line format: Executive Summary Table/Figure 1–3 became ES.1–ES.3; Introduction Tables I.1–I.4 split into label plus title.
- Typos fixed: "Mandatory  is the share", "Score follow", "workbooks's", stray "Contractual", "Note:" changed to "Note." to match.

## 4. Typography and capitalisation
- Title Case applied to 133 headings, 650 table column headers and 10 captions (ALL-CAPS headers such as TERM converted).
- Glossary font Aptos replaced with Roboto Condensed (312 font attributes), matching other tables.
- Column header "w" expanded to "Weight (w)" (5 tables).
- RACI labels (A) and "Annex A" protected from Title Case.

## 5. British spelling
- About 444 US forms converted (-ize/-ization to -ise/-isation, programme, defence, behaviour, judgement, analyse and others). Chapter title now "CDRRMO Organisational Structure...".
- Left unchanged on purpose: Emergency Operations Center / Center names, official names containing "Program" (e.g. "Budget Program and Investment Plan"), "International Organization for Standardization / Migration", and the Fogg citation and model name.

## 6. Not changed
- Edition number, version register and the 12 November 2026 date (decision for the document owner).
- Images and charts (cannot be edited as text).
- Table of Contents: set to refresh when opened in Word (choose Yes at the prompt).
