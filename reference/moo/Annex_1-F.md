# ANNEX 1-F | INTEGRATED RACI ACCOUNTABILITY FRAMEWORK

The following matrices translate the governance instruments into assigned responsibilities. They should be adopted as controlled annexes to the MOO and reviewed whenever the City's organizational structure, statutory mandates or operating arrangements change.

**RACI definitions**

- **R – Responsible:** Performs or coordinates the work.
- **A – Accountable:** Holds final responsibility for the task or decision. There should normally be one accountable role for each activity.
- **C – Consulted:** Provides technical advice or is consulted before action.
- **I – Informed:** Receives information on the action or decision.

The RACI matrix does not create legal authority. Where law assigns a decision to a specific official or body, that statutory authority prevails over any administrative assignment in the matrix.

## 6.1 CDRRMC Governance RACI

| Governance activity | City Mayor | CDRRMC | CDRRMO Secretariat | City offices | CSO / private sector |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Convene regular CDRRMC meeting | A | C | R | I | I |
| Prepare agenda and technical briefs | I | C | A/R | C | C |
| Review Local DRRM Plan | I | A | R | R | C |
| Integrate DRRM into local plans and investment programming | I | C | R | A/R\* | C |
| Adopt CDRRMC resolutions | I | A/R | R (documentation) | C | C |
| Monitor Council decisions | I | A | R | R | I |
| Update membership and focal persons | A | C | R | C | C |
| Conduct annual governance review | A | R | R | R | C |
| Recommend State of Calamity | I | A/R | R (technical consolidation) | C | I |
| Recommend SID through legally required channel | A** | R/C** | R (technical support) | C | I |

\* Each office remains accountable for its own statutory planning and technical functions. CPDO retains its role in local development and land use planning; CDRRMO provides DRRM risk information and technical integration support.

\*\* The exact SID recommendation route must be aligned with Republic Act No. 12287 and its IRR. The matrix must be revised if the certified legal text assigns the recommendation to a regional or other competent authority. The City Mayor's declaration authority is not a substitute for that prerequisite.

## 6.5 Cross-office institutional interface RACI

This matrix is designed to preserve the mandates of technical and statutory offices while ensuring that DRRM information and coordination are integrated across the City Government.

| Function / deliverable | Accountable lead | CDRRMO role | Supporting offices / partners |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| CLUP and CDP risk integration | CPDO / authorized planning authority | Provides risk data and technical review | City offices, barangays, CDRRMC |
| Hazard, exposure and vulnerability information | Competent technical data owners, coordinated through CDRRMO | Coordinates consolidation and use in DRRM planning | MGB, PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, CPDO, barangays, other agencies |
| Critical facility safety assessment | Relevant engineering / building authority | Coordinates prioritization based on risk | City Engineering, OBO, facility owners |
| Watershed and ecosystem management | CENRO / competent environmental authority | Integrates ecosystem risk into DRRM plans | DENR, barangays, community organizations |
| Public health emergency preparedness | CHO / competent health authority | Coordinates EOC and multi-sector support | Hospitals, DOH, health facilities |
| School safety and continuity | DepEd / competent education authority | Coordinates risk information and emergency interface | School heads, barangays, CHO |
| Agriculture and fisheries risk management | City Agriculture / Veterinary Office | Integrates sectoral risk and response information | BFAR, DA, farmers' and fishers' organizations |
| Business continuity | Relevant business owners / competent economic office | Facilitates coordination and continuity planning | Chamber of Commerce, DTI, utilities |
| Lifeline continuity | Respective utility or service provider | Coordinates status reporting and resource support | Water, power, telecom and transport providers |
| Social protection and evacuation assistance | CSWDO / competent social welfare authority | Coordinates risk information and EOC tracking | Barangays, health offices, DSWD, CSOs |
| Local infrastructure recovery | Relevant implementing office | Coordinates risk-informed recovery monitoring | CPDO, City Engineering, utilities, national agencies |
