# ANNEX 1-F \| MASTER RACI AND INSTITUTIONAL INTERFACE MATRIX

**PROPOSED – FOR LEGAL / TECHNICAL REVIEW**

Document status: Draft instrument. This text is a drafting aid and must be checked against the latest certified statutes, implementing rules, local organizational structure, approved plantilla, budget, and applicable City procedures before signature or enactment.

| **Code** | **Meaning** | **Application**                                                                        |
|----------|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| R        | Responsible | Performs work and produces output.                                                     |
| A        | Accountable | Owns result / decision; one A per activity, subject to collective statutory authority. |
| C        | Consulted   | Provides required advice before decision.                                              |
| I        | Informed    | Receives relevant information.                                                         |

RACI does not create statutory authority, transfer legal mandates or override formal delegations. A collective statutory body remains accountable for its decisions.
