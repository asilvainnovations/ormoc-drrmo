# Annex 5-C. Pre-disaster Recovery Framework Outline

1.  Purpose, scope and adoption (CDRRMC resolution, version, review date).

2.  Recovery principles: Build Back Better, inclusion, local ownership, privacy.

3.  Recovery baseline summary by barangay (Annex 5-E) and known data gaps.

4.  Recovery scenarios per priority hazard (most probable and most severe) and the recovery needs they imply.

5.  Critical areas, assets and population groups, in order of recovery priority.

6.  Recovery organisation: Rehabilitation and Recovery Committee, sub-committees, lead offices and focal persons.

7.  PDNA activation: triggers, decision route, team designations, templates and OCD VIII support arrangements.

8.  Temporary shelter and evacuation centre closure arrangements (with CSWDO and the housing office).

9.  Debris and post-disaster waste management (with ENRO and City Engineering).

10. Scalable and reprogrammable PPAs by office.

11. Recovery financing map: fund sources, insurance status of City assets, farmer enrolment, partner commitments.

12. Pre-agreed partnership arrangements (MOAs with private sector, CSOs, academe and suppliers; Chapter 8).

13. Monitoring unit, matrix and reporting arrangements.

14. Communication strategy template and feedback channels.

15. Annual review record and lessons from recent events.
