# Annex 5-C. Anticipatory Action Funding Pathway and Tranche Worksheet

Basis: RA 12287 and its IRR (signed 14 September 2026), Rule V Secs. 1–3; ADR-025. The official text governs. DBM–COA guidelines on AA funds and NDRRMC operational guidelines are pending; this worksheet will be revised when they are issued.

## 1. Pathway

1. **Plan.** The LDRRMFIP carries an AA line of no more than 10% of the 70% LDRRMF component, naming the AA measures (by hazard and sector), lead offices and procurement mode (SOP 5-01).
2. **Pre-arrange.** Framework agreements and supplier lists cover AA items (SOP 5-06).
3. **Declare.** The City Mayor declares a State of Imminent Disaster after the PDRA, CDRRMC recommendation and RDRRMC process (Chapter 1, SOP GOV-05).
4. **Release.** Budget, Accounting and Treasury release funds immediately, within the tranche rule (SOP 5-05).
5. **Deliver.** Lead offices carry out AA measures; No Regrets Principle applies.
6. **Lift and report.** The declaration lifts on hazard occurrence or on the Mayor's lifting; unused AA funds accrue to the STF; the 45-day report includes AA fund use.

## 3. Sources not subject to graduation

STF balances within their five-year period; funds from partners and private or international donors; ODA; donations of other LGUs; other lawful sources (IRR Rule V Secs. 1(b) and 2). Their own terms and COA rules still apply.

## 4. Slow-onset hazards

Until the NDRRMC operational guidelines are issued, drought and other slow-onset hazards are not graduated, but disbursements never exceed the year's AA line (ADR-025 item 4). Confirm with the City Legal Office and OCD VIII.
