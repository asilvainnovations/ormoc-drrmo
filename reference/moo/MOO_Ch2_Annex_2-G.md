# ANNEX 2-G \| SOP HR-01 – ANNUAL WORKFORCE PLANNING AND PLANTILLA REVIEW

**Purpose**: Ensure the CDRRMO has a lawful, risk-informed and adequately planned workforce without creating unauthorized posts or commitments.

**Trigger**: Annual planning cycle; material change in mandate, workload, risk profile, staffing or assessment results.

**Owner**: CDRRMO Head jointly with HRMO for workforce-planning controls.

**Procedure:**

1\. Division leads submit workload, service demand, outputs, critical functions and staffing availability.

2\. CDRRMO consolidates workload against LDRRMP priorities, statutory functions, operating hours and continuity requirements.

3\. HRMO certifies approved structure, plantilla, filled/vacant items, qualification standards and personnel status.

4\. Identify gaps by category: authorized vacancy, skills gap, workload imbalance, process inefficiency, equipment constraint or inter-office dependency.

5\. Develop options: internal redeployment within authority, training, process redesign, lawful temporary augmentation, or formal position/structure proposal.

6\. Budget assesses affordability and recurring costs; CLO reviews legal route; other offices review relevant impacts.

7\. Submit proposals to the competent approving authority. Do not implement unapproved structural or fiscal changes.

8\. Record approved actions, owners, deadlines and evidence; review quarterly and annually.

Records: annual workforce plan; certified plantilla; gap matrix; workload analysis; endorsements; approval; implementation and CAPA register.

Escalation: unresolved critical vacancies or unsafe coverage are elevated to the Mayor through established administrative channels, with interim risk controls documented.
