# Annex 6-C. Anticipatory Action Funding Pathway and Tranche Worksheet

Basis: RA 12287 and its IRR (signed 14 September 2026), Rule V Secs. 1–3; ADR-025. The official text governs. DBM–COA guidelines on AA funds and NDRRMC operational guidelines are pending; this worksheet will be revised when they are issued.

1.  **Plan.** The LDRRMFIP carries an AA line of no more than 10% of the 70% component, naming the AA measures (by hazard and sector), lead offices and procurement mode (SOP 6-01).

2.  **Pre-arrange.** Framework agreements, supplier lists and stock plans cover AA items (SOPs 6-06, 6-11).

3.  **Declare.** The City Mayor declares a State of Imminent Disaster after the PDRA, CDRRMC recommendation and RDRRMC process (Chapter 1, SOP GOV-05).

4.  **Release.** Budget, Accounting and Treasury release funds immediately, within the tranche rule (SOP 6-05).

5.  **Deliver.** Lead offices carry out AA measures; the No Regrets Principle applies.

6.  **Lift and report.** The declaration lifts on hazard occurrence or on the Mayor's lifting; unused AA funds accrue to the STF; the 45-day report includes AA fund use (Chapter 7).
