# SOP 1 \- GOV-05: Standard Operating Procedure for Declaration of a State of Imminent Disaster and Implementation of Anticipatory Actions
SOP-DRRM-GOV-05 · Draft for validation against Republic Act No. 12287, its certified IRR and applicable funding rules

## 5.1 Purpose
This SOP establishes the City's procedures for identifying an imminent disaster threat, evaluating credible forecasts and impact information, recommending a State of Imminent Disaster (SID), supporting the City Mayor's declaration by Executive Order, implementing anticipatory actions, managing resources and documenting results.

The purpose is to enable timely action before disaster impacts occur, where credible forecasts, warnings and risk information indicate that a hazard may cause serious impacts within a defined period.

## 5.2 Legal Basis and Legal Safeguards
This SOP shall be implemented in accordance with Republic Act No. 12287, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and other applicable laws and issuances.

The SOP shall preserve the following distinctions:

1. A State of Imminent Disaster is a legal declaration relating to an impending disaster threat under the statutory framework.
2. A Pre-Disaster Risk Assessment (PDRA) is a technical process for assessing the potential impacts of a forecast or anticipated hazard.
3. Anticipatory actions are time-bound actions implemented before the anticipated impacts to reduce expected loss and harm.
4. The declaration and anticipatory actions shall be based on the applicable statutory recommendation, evidence and procedural requirements.
5. The City Mayor's authority to declare by Executive Order shall be exercised only upon the recommendation required by law. The exact role and sequence of the Regional DRRM Council and other competent authorities must be followed.
6. The declaration does not dispense with applicable budget, procurement, accounting, auditing, data privacy or other legal controls.

Important validation requirement: Before this SOP is adopted, the City Legal Office and CDRRMO must verify the certified text of Republic Act No. 12287 and its IRR, particularly the provisions on the recommending authority, declaration requirements, anticipatory action protocols, fund eligibility, lead times, social amelioration, data sharing and post-lifting reporting. The assessment workbook records specific requirements, including a 45-day post-lifting report and certain fund-use and tranche provisions. Those details must be checked against the certified IRR and applicable DBM and COA rules before they are incorporated into binding city procedures.

## 5.3 Scope
This SOP applies to the City Mayor, CDRRMC, CDRRMO, EOC, City Government departments, barangays, relevant national government agencies, utilities, CSOs and other partners involved in anticipatory action.

It applies to hazards for which credible forecasts, warnings or other recognized technical information can provide sufficient lead time for protective action.

## 5.4 Operational Principles
* Forecast-informed: Use authoritative forecasts and warnings from competent technical agencies.
* Impact-based: Assess who and what may be affected, where, when and how severely.
* Action-oriented: Link every trigger to a defined protective action, responsible office, resource requirement and deadline.
* Time-critical: Establish decision and implementation deadlines based on the available lead time.
* Inclusive: Address the needs of at-risk households and sectors, including people who may face barriers to receiving warnings or evacuating.
* Accountable: Record the evidence, recommendation, declaration, funding, implementation and results.
* Proportionate: Match actions and resource commitments to the forecast risk and applicable legal authority.

## 5.5 Hazard-specific anticipatory action protocols
The CDRRMO shall coordinate the development and maintenance of hazard-specific protocols. These shall be based on the City's risk profile and the official warning and forecasting systems of competent agencies.

At a minimum, protocols should cover:

| Hazard | Technical information to monitor | Potential anticipatory actions |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Tropical cyclone and extreme rainfall | PAGASA bulletins, rainfall forecasts, wind signals, track and impact forecasts | Prepositioning, evacuation readiness, suspension recommendations, protection of facilities and lifelines |
| Riverine flooding, including relevant river systems such as Anilao and Malbasag | Rainfall forecasts, river levels, upstream conditions, local gauges and flood models where available | Targeted warnings, pre-emptive evacuation where justified, deployment of rescue assets, protection of critical facilities |
| Rain-induced landslide | PAGASA rainfall information, MGB susceptibility information, field observations and local rainfall thresholds | Targeted warnings, access restrictions, evacuation and relocation of people in threatened areas |
| Tsunami affecting Ormoc Bay and coastal areas | PHIVOLCS tsunami bulletins, earthquake information, sea-level observations and official advisories | Immediate warning dissemination, evacuation to designated safe areas, closure of exposed coastal access points |
| Other locally relevant hazards | Authoritative agency warnings and validated local monitoring | Actions defined in the relevant hazard-specific protocol |

The City shall not invent or independently substitute technical warning thresholds for those issued by competent national agencies. Locally developed thresholds may supplement official warnings only when technically validated, documented and approved through the appropriate process.

## 5.6 Roles and responsibilities
| Actor | Responsibilities |
| ----- | ----- |
| City Mayor | Exercises the statutory declaration authority by EO upon the recommendation required by law; provides executive direction and authorizes actions within lawful authority |
| Regional DRRM Council / competent recommending authority | Performs the recommendation or coordination role prescribed by Republic Act No. 12287 and its IRR |
| CDRRMC | Provides local coordination, reviews the PDRA and anticipatory action recommendations, and mobilizes institutional support within its mandate |
| CDRRMO | Coordinates PDRA, prepares technical recommendations, maintains hazard protocols and monitors implementation |
| EOC | Monitors forecasts, maintains COP, disseminates authorized instructions, tracks actions and resources, and prepares reports |
| Barangays | Validate local conditions, receive and relay warnings, identify at-risk households, implement assigned local actions and report completion |
| City Social Welfare and Development Office | Validates social protection needs and coordinates anticipatory social amelioration with relevant offices |
| City Health Office | Coordinates health-related protective actions and continuity of essential health services |
| City Engineering / Building Official | Advises on infrastructure safety, access, structural risks and protective measures within mandate |
| City Agriculture / Veterinary Office | Assesses agricultural, livestock and fisheries exposure and coordinates sector-specific protective measures |
| CPDO | Supports spatial analysis, exposure assessment and integration with local development information |
| City Budget Office / Treasurer / Accounting / BAC / relevant offices | Ensure that funding, procurement, disbursement and accountability arrangements comply with applicable rules |
| Utilities and private sector partners | Implement agreed continuity and protective measures within their respective responsibilities |

## 5.7 Procedure
### Step 1. Continuous monitoring and threat identification
The EOC shall monitor official forecasts, warnings, bulletins and relevant field information.

The duty officer shall record:

* Source agency;
* Bulletin or advisory number;
* Date and time of issuance;
* Hazard type;
* Forecast location and affected areas;
* Expected timing;
* Severity or intensity information;
* Forecast confidence or uncertainty, where provided;
* Potentially exposed population and assets; and
* Recommended monitoring or readiness actions.

### Step 2. Initial threat screening
The CDRRMO shall conduct an initial screening to determine whether the threat requires:

* Routine monitoring;
* Enhanced monitoring;
* Activation of the PDRA process;
* Pre-positioning or readiness actions under existing authority; or
* Consideration of a State of Imminent Disaster.

The City shall not delay urgent protective actions that are already authorized and warranted merely because the SID declaration process is being considered.

### Step 3. Convening the PDRA process
Where the threat warrants formal assessment, the CDRRMO shall convene the relevant technical offices and agencies.

The PDRA shall identify:

1. Hazard characteristics and forecast scenario;
2. Geographic areas potentially affected;
3. Exposed population and vulnerable groups;
4. Potential impacts on housing, infrastructure, lifelines and essential services;
5. Potential livelihood and economic impacts;
6. Evacuation and access constraints;
7. Critical facilities and essential services at risk;
8. Potential secondary or cascading impacts;
9. Existing response and coping capacities;
10. Priority anticipatory actions; and
11. Required resources, lead times and responsible offices.

The PDRA shall clearly identify assumptions, uncertainties, source information and the time at which the assessment was prepared.

### Step 4. Development of the Anticipatory Action Matrix
The CDRRMO shall coordinate the preparation of an action matrix that links each identified risk to a concrete action.

The matrix shall include:

| Required field | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| Hazard / scenario | The forecast hazard and relevant scenario |
| Trigger | Official warning, forecast threshold or validated local trigger |
| Geographic coverage | Barangays, communities, facilities or sectors concerned |
| Anticipatory action | Specific action to be implemented before impact |
| Responsible office | Office with operational responsibility |
| Supporting offices | Offices or partners providing support |
| Target completion | Deadline based on lead time |
| Resource requirement | Personnel, equipment, supplies, transport and funding |
| Approval authority | Official or body authorized to approve the action |
| Evidence of completion | Log, acknowledgment, dispatch record, photograph or other proof |
| Status | Not started, ongoing, completed, delayed or cancelled |

Actions may include:

* Pre-positioning of food, water, medicines, rescue assets and other essential supplies;
* Activation of evacuation centers and transport arrangements;
* Pre-emptive evacuation where justified by the risk and authorized procedure;
* Protection of critical infrastructure and essential services;
* Public warnings and targeted advisories;
* Assistance to people requiring mobility or medical support;
* Protection of livestock, agricultural assets and livelihoods where feasible;
* Continuity measures for health, education, water and other essential services; and
* Social amelioration or other assistance in accordance with applicable law and eligibility rules.

### Step 5. Review and recommendation
The CDRRMO shall submit the PDRA and proposed Anticipatory Action Matrix to the CDRRMC or the appropriate authority for review and recommendation, following the statutory process.

The review shall establish:

* Whether the threat meets the legal basis for consideration of an SID;
* Whether the forecast and impact evidence is sufficiently credible;
* Whether the anticipated impacts justify the proposed declaration;
* Whether proposed actions can be completed within the available lead time;
* Whether funding and resources are legally available;
* Whether affected populations can be reached through warning and assistance systems; and
* Whether the recommended actions are within the authority of the responsible offices.

The recommendation shall be formally documented and transmitted through the legally required channel.

### Step 6. Declaration by the City Mayor
Upon receipt of the recommendation required by law, the City Mayor may issue an Executive Order declaring a State of Imminent Disaster, consistent with Republic Act No. 12287 and its IRR.

The EO shall identify:

* Legal basis;
* Hazard and forecast scenario;
* Geographic coverage;
* Date and time of effectivity;
* Technical basis and recommendation reference;
* Authorized anticipatory action arrangements;
* Responsible offices and coordination mechanisms;
* Resource and reporting arrangements; and
* Review, extension or lifting arrangements, as permitted by law.

The declaration shall not be issued solely on the basis of an unsupported forecast or an unverified social media report.

### Step 7. Activation and dissemination
Upon issuance of the EO:

1. The EOC shall record the declaration and disseminate it to concerned offices and barangays.
2. The CDRRMO shall activate the relevant hazard-specific protocol.
3. Responsible offices shall implement their assigned actions.
4. Barangays shall acknowledge receipt of warnings and instructions.
5. The EOC shall maintain a live action tracker and COP.
6. Public information shall be issued through authorized channels.

### Step 8. Implementation of anticipatory actions
Each responsible office shall implement the actions assigned in the approved matrix.

The EOC shall track:

* Action;
* Responsible office;
* Target deadline;
* Actual completion time;
* Geographic coverage;
* Number and type of people reached;
* Resources deployed;
* Outstanding constraints; and
* Evidence of completion.

If a planned action cannot be completed, the responsible office shall report the reason, risk implications and alternative measures.

### Step 9. Anticipatory social amelioration
Where social amelioration is included in the approved action package, the responsible social welfare office shall coordinate beneficiary identification, eligibility validation, assistance delivery and reporting.

The process shall:

* Use the applicable beneficiary registry or authorized data source;
* Verify eligibility and avoid duplication;
* Prioritize households facing heightened exposure and barriers to self-protection;
* Maintain clear records of assistance;
* Apply data minimization and access controls; and
* Ensure lawful data sharing in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 and applicable rules.

The CDRRMO and EOC shall coordinate information and operational requirements but shall not independently determine social welfare eligibility outside the authority of the responsible office.

### Step 10. Monitoring and adjustment
The EOC shall monitor changes in the forecast, official warnings and actual field conditions.

The CDRRMO shall recommend adjustments to the action matrix when:

* The forecast changes materially;
* The hazard's timing or geographic coverage changes;
* New areas become exposed;
* A protective action becomes unsafe or impracticable;
* Additional resources become necessary; or
* The threat diminishes and planned actions should be modified or stood down.

Changes shall be recorded with the time, decision-maker, rationale and offices notified.

### Step 11. Lifting or termination of the SID
The SID shall be lifted or otherwise terminated in accordance with the applicable statutory requirements and the authority prescribed by Republic Act No. 12287 and its IRR.

The CDRRMO shall prepare a technical recommendation based on:

* Updated official forecasts and warnings;
* Actual hazard development;
* Residual risk;
* Completion or cancellation of anticipatory actions;
* Continuing safety requirements; and
* The legal requirements for lifting.

The City Mayor shall issue the appropriate instrument where authorized and required by law.

### Step 12. Post-lifting report and review
The CDRRMO shall coordinate the preparation and submission of the required post-lifting report within the period prescribed by law and the IRR. The assessment workbook identifies a 45-day post-lifting reporting requirement; the City Legal Office must confirm the precise trigger, recipient, required contents and computation of the reporting period against the certified IRR before this is adopted as a binding deadline.

The report should document:

* Forecast and warning chronology;
* Basis for the PDRA and declaration;
* Anticipatory actions planned and implemented;
* Offices and partners involved;
* Population and sectors reached;
* Resources and funds used;
* Actions not completed and reasons;
* Actual hazard impacts compared with the forecast scenario;
* Lessons learned;
* Recommendations; and
* Corrective actions.

## 5.9 Minimum Records
* Official warning and forecast bulletins;
* EOC monitoring log;
* PDRA report;
* Exposure and impact maps;
* Anticipatory Action Matrix;
* CDRRMC or competent authority recommendation;
* City Mayor's Executive Order;
* Public advisories and barangay acknowledgments;
* Resource prepositioning and deployment records;
* Evacuation and assistance records;
* Social amelioration records, subject to privacy safeguards;
* EOC situation reports and action tracker;
* Lifting or termination instrument;
* Post-lifting report; and
* After-Action Review and Improvement Plan.
