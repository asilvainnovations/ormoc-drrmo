## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE GOV-05: STATE OF IMMINENT DISASTER DECLARATION, ANTICIPATORY ACTION, LIFTING AND POST-LIFTING REPORTING

Purpose. To establish a controlled process under RA 12287 and its certified IRR for PDRA, regional recommendation, local declaration, anticipatory action, lifting and reporting.

Critical authority rule. A forecast alone does not automatically justify declaration. Statutory criteria, PDRA requirements and prescribed recommendation / coordination mechanism must be met. The Mayor's declaration authority shall be exercised only after all legal prerequisites are confirmed.

| **Control**              | **Requirement**                                                                                            |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Process owner            | CDRRMO Head                                                                                                |
| Technical lead           | CDRRMO Operations and Warning with sector offices                                                          |
| Regional interface       | OCD Region VIII / RDRRMC VIII, subject to certified IRR                                                    |
| Local decision authority | City Mayor, through EO only after prescribed recommendation                                                |
| Funding                  | Budget / Accounting / Procurement subject to certified IRR and DBM / COA rules                             |
| Reporting                | Post-lifting deadline as prescribed by certified IRR; 45 days reported in assessment and must be confirmed |

### Procedure

1\. Monitor official forecasts and advisories from PAGASA, PHIVOLCS and other competent authorities; log source, issue time, validity, geography and lead time.

2\. Initiate PDRA when hazard-specific triggers warrant; assess exposure, vulnerability, projected severity, cascading impacts, sectoral needs, critical facilities, lead time and response capacity.

3\. Obtain sector inputs from health, social welfare, agriculture, engineering, utilities, education, transport, environment and barangays as relevant.

4\. Prepare PDRA summary, proposed anticipatory measures, resource requirements, funding source, implementation owners, safeguards and indicators.

5\. Convene CDRRMC or its authorized mechanism to review evidence and endorse the City's request, without substituting for the statutory regional recommendation.

6\. Coordinate with OCD Region VIII / RDRRMC VIII through the certified IRR route; log request, attachments, receipt, clarifications, recommendation and official response.

7\. After confirmation of prerequisites, prepare the Mayor's EO specifying hazard, covered areas, validity, authorized measures, responsible offices, resource controls and reporting.

8\. Implement only approved and legally authorized actions, which may include prepositioning, early evacuation, critical infrastructure protection, continuity measures and targeted assistance.

9\. Maintain action logs, beneficiary records, procurement and disbursement records, coverage, outputs, complaints and safeguards. Apply RA 10173 safeguards.

10\. Monitor hazard and impacts; lift the declaration in the manner prescribed by law and document basis, effective time and transition arrangements.

11\. Prepare and submit required post-lifting report within deadline in certified IRR; assessment records a 45-day period, which must be verified.

12\. Conduct after-action review and update triggers, PDRA tools, SOP and corrective action register.

Mandatory hold points: CLO verifies certified RA 12287 IRR and recommendation route; OCD VIII confirms regional / provincial coordination applicability to Ormoc as an Independent Component City; Budget, Accounting, CLO and OCD verify reported allocation percentages and tranche rules against certified IRR and DBM/COA guidance; beneficiary data comply with RA 10173.

Required records: official forecast log; PDRA; sector inputs; CDRRMC records; regional request and acknowledgment; recommendation; Mayor's EO; anticipatory action / expenditure logs; lifting documentation; post-lifting report; after-action review.
