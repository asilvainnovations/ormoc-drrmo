# ANNEX 1-A \| PROPOSED ORDINANCE – CDRRMO INSTITUTIONALIZATION

**PROPOSED – FOR LEGAL / TECHNICAL REVIEW**

Document status: Draft instrument. This text is a drafting aid and must be checked against the latest certified statutes, implementing rules, local organizational structure, approved plantilla, budget, and applicable City procedures before signature or enactment.

## AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING AND STRENGTHENING THE CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT OFFICE OF ORMOC CITY, DEFINING ITS ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE, FUNCTIONS AND ACCOUNTABILITY, PROVIDING FOR COORDINATION WITH THE CDRRMC AND EOC, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Ormoc City, in session assembled, that:

## Section 1. Short Title.

This Ordinance shall be known as the “Ormoc City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office Institutionalization Ordinance.”

## Section 2. Declaration of Policy.

The City shall maintain a permanent, adequately staffed, technically competent and accountable CDRRMO consistent with RA 10121, its IRR, JMC No. 2014-1 and applicable local government, civil service and budget rules.

## Section 3. Establishment and Status.

The CDRRMO is hereby established or, if already established by prior valid issuance, strengthened and institutionalized as a regular office of the City Government. The City Legal Office shall determine whether this is a new or amendatory ordinance after reviewing existing issuances.

## Section 4. Organizational Structure.

The CDRRMO shall be headed by a duly qualified CDRRMO Head and maintain the functional areas required by applicable national guidelines: Administration and Training; Research and Planning; and Operations and Warning. Additional units and positions require approval under DBM, CSC and City processes.

## Section 5. Functions.

The CDRRMO shall lead and coordinate local DRRM planning and implementation; risk information; contingency planning; early warning; training; preparedness; response coordination; recovery support; CDRRMC Secretariat functions; reporting; and institutional performance improvement, consistent with RA 10121.

## Section 6. Relationship with CDRRMC and EOC.

The CDRRMC remains the City's statutory policy-making, coordinating and oversight body. The CDRRMO is its permanent technical and administrative office. The EOC is an operational coordination mechanism established through a separate executive issuance. It does not replace the CDRRMO or field ICS command.

## Section 7. Coordination and non-duplication.

The CDRRMO shall coordinate with City offices, barangays, national agencies, utilities, CSOs and private-sector partners, but shall not assume technical, regulatory, fiduciary or statutory functions assigned to other offices.

## Section 8. Staffing and qualifications.

HRMO and CDRRMO shall prepare staffing, qualification standards and position descriptions for review under applicable CSC and DBM rules. No position is created beyond an approved plantilla and lawful appropriation.

## Section 9. Funding.

Funding shall be sourced from lawful appropriations and eligible DRRM funds, subject to RA 10121, LDRRMF guidelines, budgeting, accounting, procurement and audit rules. This Ordinance does not independently authorize a particular allocation or expenditure.

## Section 10. Implementing procedures.

The CDRRMO shall maintain the CDRRMO–EOC Manual of Operations and implementing procedures, subject to legal review and approval by the competent authority.

## Section 11. Separability.

If any provision is declared invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain effective.

## Section 12. Repealing / amending clause.

Inconsistent local issuances are amended or repealed only to the extent of inconsistency, subject to identification by the City Legal Office.

## Section 13. Effectivity.

This Ordinance shall take effect in accordance with the Local Government Code and applicable approval, posting and publication requirements.
