**AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT OFFICE (CDRRMO) AS A REGULAR DEPARTMENT OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF ORMOC, DEFINING ITS ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE, FUNCTIONS, STAFFING, COORDINATION MECHANISMS AND ACCOUNTABILITY SYSTEM, PROVIDING APPROPRIATIONS THEREFOR, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES**

### Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of Ormoc City, in session assembled:
### Section 1. Title
This Ordinance shall be known as the “Ormoc City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office Institutionalization Ordinance.”

### Section 2. Declaration of Policy
It is hereby declared the policy of the City Government of Ormoc to institutionalize an integrated, risk-informed, inclusive and accountable local disaster risk reduction and management system that protects lives, livelihoods, assets, ecosystems and essential services; reduces disaster risks; strengthens climate resilience; and supports preparedness, response, recovery and sustainable development.

The City shall pursue a whole-of-government and whole-of-society approach, recognizing that disaster risk is shaped by the interaction of hazards, exposure, vulnerability, capacity, land use, infrastructure, ecosystems, governance and socioeconomic conditions.

The City shall mainstream DRRM and climate change adaptation into local development planning, investment programming, budgeting, land use management, infrastructure development, social protection and public service delivery.

### Section 3. Legal Basis
This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to, and shall be interpreted consistently with, the following:

1. Republic Act No. 10121, otherwise known as the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations;
2. Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991;
3. Republic Act No. 9729, or the Climate Change Act of 2009, as amended by Republic Act No. 10174;
4. Republic Act No. 11038, or the Expanded National Integrated Protected Areas System Act, where applicable to local ecosystem and protected-area concerns;
5. Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012;
6. Republic Act No. 12287, or the law providing for the declaration of a State of Imminent Disaster and the implementation of anticipatory actions, together with its applicable IRR and subsequent issuances;
7. The applicable Department of Budget and Management, Civil Service Commission, Commission on Audit, Office of Civil Defense and other national government rules; and
8. Other relevant national laws, administrative issuances and local ordinances.

Legal drafting note: The City Legal Office shall verify the complete and current titles, provisions, implementing rules and effectivity of the cited laws and issuances before enactment. References to specific funding percentages, lead times, eligibility conditions or documentary requirements under Republic Act No. 12287 and its IRR must be checked against certified official texts and applicable DBM and COA rules.

### Section 4. Establishment and Institutional Status
Thereby institutionalized the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) as a regular department of the City Government of Ormoc, subject to the applicable requirements on local government organizational structures, staffing patterns, position classification, compensation, budgeting and personnel administration.

The CDRRMO shall serve as the City's principal technical and administrative office for the coordination, integration, implementation, monitoring and continuous improvement of local DRRM and climate change adaptation programs.

The CDRRMO shall operate under the supervision of the City Mayor, consistent with the Local Government Code, Republic Act No. 10121 and other applicable laws.

Its institutionalization shall not diminish the statutory authority of the City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (CDRRMC), the Sangguniang Panlungsod, the City Mayor, national government agencies or other city departments and offices.

### Section 5. Mandate and Objectives
The CDRRMO shall:

a. 	Develop, implement, coordinate and monitor the City's comprehensive DRRM programs and plans;
b. 	Support the integration of DRRM and climate change adaptation into local development, land use, investment and budget processes;
c. 	Maintain and periodically update risk information, hazard and exposure datasets, vulnerability assessments and risk-informed planning inputs in coordination with competent offices and agencies;
d. 	Strengthen disaster preparedness, early warning, emergency communications, response readiness and recovery planning;
e. 	Provide technical and Secretariat support to the CDRRMC;
f. 	Maintain the City's disaster operations and coordination capability, including the EOC, in accordance with approved activation and operating protocols;
g. 	Facilitate inter-office, inter-barangay, inter-agency, civil society, private sector and community coordination;
h. 	Support the organization, training, equipping and readiness of local response teams and volunteer groups;
i. 	Coordinate the conduct of post-disaster needs assessments, damage and loss assessments, and recovery planning with the responsible offices and agencies;
j. 	Monitor the implementation and performance of local DRRM programs, projects and activities;
k. 	Maintain institutional records, operational reports, risk information and lessons learned; and
l. 	Perform other functions assigned by law or validly delegated by the City Mayor, consistent with the mandate of the Office.

### Section 6. Core Functional Divisions
Subject to the approved organizational structure and staffing pattern, the CDRRMO shall be organized around the following functional areas:

1. Administration and Finance – personnel administration, procurement coordination, financial and budget management, property and logistics records, administrative support and office continuity.
2. Research, Planning and Risk Assessment – risk assessment, hazard and exposure information, vulnerability analysis, DRRM and CCA planning, policy development, and mainstreaming into local plans and investments.
3. Operations and Early Warning – preparedness, early warning coordination, EOC operations, incident information management, response coordination, drills and exercises.
4. Training, Public Information and Community Resilience – capacity development, public education, risk communication, community-based DRRM, volunteer coordination and inclusive preparedness.

The functional areas may be implemented through divisions, sections or units, as authorized by the approved organizational structure and staffing pattern. The City shall not create plantilla positions or confer statutory powers solely by virtue of this Ordinance where separate approval or compliance is required.

### Section 7. Head of Office
The CDRRMO shall be headed by a City DRRM Officer, appointed in accordance with applicable civil service laws and regulations, subject to the prescribed qualification standards, salary grade, plantilla authorization and other requirements.

The City DRRM Officer shall:

a. 	Lead the administration and technical direction of the CDRRMO;
b. 	Ensure implementation of approved DRRM policies, plans, programs and projects;
c. 	Advise the City Mayor and CDRRMC on risk, preparedness, response and recovery matters;
d. 	Serve as the designated CDRRMC Secretariat head or principal Secretariat coordinator, as provided in the applicable CDRRMC issuance;
e. 	Ensure that the EOC is maintained and operated in accordance with approved protocols;
f. 	Coordinate with national government agencies, neighboring local government units, barangays and partner institutions;
g. 	Ensure proper use, documentation and reporting of DRRM resources; and
h. 	Submit periodic performance, risk and readiness reports to the City Mayor and CDRRMC.

### Section 8. Staffing and Competency Requirements
The City Government shall develop and maintain a staffing pattern appropriate to the City's risk profile, population, geographic conditions, service demands and operational requirements.

The staffing pattern shall be consistent with applicable national guidelines, including the relevant joint issuances on LDRRMO organization and staffing, and shall be submitted for the required review and approval.

The City shall progressively establish competency-based staffing and development arrangements for the following functions:

* Risk assessment and geospatial information management;
* DRRM planning and policy integration;
* EOC operations and information management;
* Early warning and risk communication;
* Emergency logistics and resource tracking;
* Training, exercises and community preparedness;
* Recovery planning and monitoring; and
* Administration, finance and records management.

The City shall ensure that position descriptions, qualifications, competency requirements, reporting relationships and performance indicators are documented and periodically reviewed.

### Section 9. Continuous Operations and Personnel Welfare
The CDRRMO shall maintain arrangements for continuous operational readiness, including an approved duty roster, contact directory, succession and relief arrangements, office continuity procedures and EOC activation support.

The City shall provide appropriate personnel safety and welfare measures, subject to applicable laws and available appropriations. These shall include risk-appropriate personal protective equipment, occupational safety measures, responder insurance or other lawful protection mechanisms, medical support, rest and rotation arrangements, and psychosocial support.

### Section 10. Coordination with the CDRRMC
The CDRRMO shall provide technical and Secretariat support to the CDRRMC, including:

a. 	Preparation of meeting agendas and technical briefs;
b. 	Consolidation of office and barangay DRRM reports;
c. 	Documentation of resolutions, decisions and action points;
d. 	Monitoring of CDRRMC decisions and commitments;
e. 	Preparation of policy and investment recommendations; and
f. 	Maintenance of the official CDRRMC records.

The CDRRMO shall not substitute for the collective deliberative and decision-making authority of the CDRRMC.

### Section 11. Relationship with the Emergency Operations Center
The City EOC shall operate as the City's designated facility and coordination mechanism for monitoring, information consolidation, resource coordination, decision support and operational reporting during emergencies and other authorized activation periods.

The EOC shall be managed and supported by the CDRRMO under the separate EOC Establishment Executive Order and the EOC Manual of Operations.

The EOC shall not be construed as a separate local government department or as an independent incident command authority.

When an Incident Command System is activated, the EOC shall support coordination, information management, resource support and policy-level decision requirements while the Incident Commander retains incident-level command and control within the scope of the activated ICS organization.

### Section 12. Inter-Office and External Coordination
All City Government departments, offices and instrumentalities shall designate DRRM focal persons and provide relevant information and technical support within their respective mandates.

The CDRRMO shall facilitate coordination but shall not assume the statutory, regulatory or technical responsibilities of other offices.

Examples include:

* CPDO for land use, development planning and investment programming;
* City Engineering Office and Office of the Building Official for infrastructure and building safety matters;
* City Health Office and health facilities for public health and medical response;
* CENRO and relevant DENR offices for environmental and ecosystem management;
* City Agriculture and Veterinary Office for agriculture, livestock and fisheries concerns;
* DepEd and other education authorities for school safety and education continuity;
* utilities and service providers for essential service continuity; and
* private sector, business organizations and civil society partners for continuity planning, logistics, volunteer support and community resilience.

### Section 13. Planning, Budgeting and Resource Mobilization
The CDRRMO shall coordinate the preparation, updating and monitoring of the Local DRRM Plan and related investment and preparedness programs.

The City shall ensure that DRRM and CCA priorities are reflected in the appropriate local development plans, annual investment programs, annual budgets and other planning and financing instruments.

The Local DRRM Fund and other legally available resources shall be programmed, obligated, disbursed and accounted for in accordance with Republic Act No. 10121, Republic Act No. 12287 where applicable, and the relevant DBM, COA and other government rules.

Nothing in this Ordinance shall be construed as authorizing expenditure, fund transfer or procurement outside the applicable budget, accounting, auditing and procurement requirements.

### Section 14. Performance Monitoring and Institutional Accountability
The CDRRMO shall establish a performance monitoring and evaluation system based on measurable indicators, verifiable evidence and periodic management review.

At a minimum, the system shall monitor:

a. 	Institutional readiness and compliance;
b.	Implementation of the Local DRRM Plan;
c. 	EOC operational readiness and activation performance;
d. 	Early warning coverage, reach and timeliness;
e. 	Training, drills and response capability;
f. 	Resource availability, deployment and accountability;
g. 	Implementation of corrective actions and lessons learned; and
h. 	Recovery planning and implementation progress.

The CDRRMO shall submit an annual institutional performance report to the City Mayor and CDRRMC, with appropriate public disclosure consistent with applicable transparency and data privacy rules.

### Section 15. Records and Information Management
The CDRRMO shall maintain an organized records management system covering policies, plans, risk assessments, meeting records, EOC logs, warning records, resource inventories, incident reports, assessments, financial and procurement documents, after-action reviews and performance reports.

Sensitive personal information and operationally restricted information shall be handled in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 and applicable records management and disclosure rules.

### Section 16. Transition and Institutional Strengthening
Within one hundred twenty (120) days from the effectivity of this Ordinance, the City Mayor, through the CDRRMO and concerned offices, shall submit to the Sangguniang Panlungsod, as applicable:

1. The proposed organizational structure and staffing pattern;
2. A position description and competency framework;
3. A transition and capacity development plan;
4. A proposed annual work and financial plan;
5. A CDRRMO–EOC operating and coordination framework; and
6. A proposed implementation schedule for the MOO and supporting SOPs.

The City shall prioritize the correction of high-priority institutional gaps identified in the institutional assessment, subject to lawful authority, technical validation and available resources.

### Section 17. Appropriations
The amount necessary for the initial implementation of this Ordinance shall be charged against available appropriations of the City Government, subject to applicable budgeting, accounting and auditing rules.

Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary shall be included in the annual city budget, subject to the usual budgetary process.

### Section 18. Implementing Rules and Regulations
Within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Ordinance, the City Mayor, upon recommendation of the CDRRMO and in consultation with the concerned offices, shall issue the necessary implementing rules and administrative guidelines, consistent with this Ordinance and applicable national laws.

### Section 19. Separability Clause
If any provision of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue in full force and effect.

### Section 20. Repealing and Amending Clause
All local ordinances, resolutions, executive orders, rules and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed, amended or modified accordingly, to the extent of such inconsistency.

### Section 21. Effectivity
This Ordinance shall take effect in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Local Government Code and other laws governing the enactment and effectivity of local ordinances.
