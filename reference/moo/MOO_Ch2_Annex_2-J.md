# ANNEX 2-J \| SOP HR-04 – COMPETENCY ASSESSMENT AND LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT

**Purpose**: Build demonstrable capability for risk reduction, preparedness, coordination and safe performance.

**Owner**: CDRRMO Head; HRMO validates agency L&D and competency-based HR alignment.

**Procedure**:

1\. Extract competencies from approved position profiles and operational task requirements.

2\. Assess current capability using records, structured assessment, observed performance, exercises and supervisor validation.

3\. Identify proficiency gaps and prioritize by life-safety, legal compliance, criticality and likelihood of deployment.

4\. Prepare an annual L&D plan with objectives, target participants, prerequisites, provider, budget, schedule and evaluation method.

5\. Obtain HRMO and budget review and required approvals.

6\. Deliver training, coaching, mentoring, drills or supervised practice.

7\. Evaluate learning and demonstrated competence; attendance or certificates alone are insufficient for high-risk task authorization.

8\. Update competency records and duty eligibility; schedule refresher or remediation.

9\. Review outcomes and revise the plan using exercise and incident lessons.

Records: competency matrix; TNA; approved L&D plan; attendance; assessment; certificates; exercise evaluation; authorization/qualification register.
