# ANNEX 1-H | IMPLEMENTATION AND INSTITUTIONALIZATION ROADMAP

The package should be implemented as a sequenced institutional reform program rather than as a collection of separate documents.

## 1. First 30 days: legal and institutional validation

| Priority action | Lead | Supporting offices | Required output |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Review existing CDRRMO ordinance / EO and staffing structure | City Legal Office / CDRRMO | HRMO, Budget Office | Legal and institutional gap analysis |
| Validate statutory CDRRMC composition | CDRRMO Secretariat | City Legal Office, CSOs, private sector | Updated membership register |
| Review existing EOC issuances and arrangements | CDRRMO | City Legal Office, concerned offices | EOC legal and operational gap analysis |
| Validate RA 12287 and IRR requirements | City Legal Office | CDRRMO, Budget Office, Accounting, Treasurer | SID legal compliance checklist |
| Reconcile assessment workbook findings | CDRRMO | Concerned technical offices | Validated institutional assessment |

## 2. Days 31–60: adoption and issuance

| Priority action | Lead | Required output |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Finalize and submit CDRRMO Ordinance | City Mayor / CDRRMO | Ordinance proposal submitted to SP |
| Finalize updated CDRRMC EO | City Legal Office / Secretariat | Signed EO and membership annex |
| Finalize EOC Establishment EO | City Legal Office / CDRRMO | Signed EO and activation framework |
| Approve CDRRMC Secretariat SOP | CDRRMO Secretariat | Approved Secretariat SOP |
| Approve EOC activation and reporting forms | CDRRMO | Controlled EOC forms |

## 3. Days 61–90: operational readiness

| Priority action | Lead | Required output |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Finalize State of Calamity SOP | CDRRMO / City Legal Office | Approved SOP |
| Finalize SID SOP and hazard protocols | CDRRMO / City Legal Office | Approved SOP and action matrices |

| Establish resource inventory and tracking | CDRRMO / General Services / Budget | Updated resource register |
| Conduct orientation for offices and barangays | CDRRMO | Attendance and competency records |

## 4. Days 91–120: testing and institutional assurance

| Priority action | Lead | Required output |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Conduct EOC functional exercise | CDRRMO | Exercise report |
| Test State of Calamity assessment process | CDRRMO / technical offices | Assessment simulation report |
| Test SID PDRA and anticipatory action process | CDRRMO / concerned offices | PDRA simulation and action tracker |
| Test last-mile communication | CDRRMO / barangays | Communications test results |

| Integrate approved instruments into MOO | CDRRMO | Controlled MOO version |

## 5. Institutional performance indicators

The following indicators are recommended for the first implementation year. Baselines should be established during the initial 30-day validation period.

| Performance area | Indicator | Suggested initial target |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Legal institutionalization | CDRRMO ordinance submitted and acted upon through the prescribed process | 100% completion of required legislative steps |
| Governance | CDRRMC membership and committee directory validated | 100% |
| Governance | Quarterly CDRRMC meetings conducted and documented | 4 per year |
| Secretariat | Council action points with assigned owners and deadlines | 100% |
| EOC | Approved EOC activation and deactivation SOP | 1 approved SOP |
| EOC | Duty roster and alternate coverage established | 100% of required posts |
| Early warning | Barangays with designated warning focal persons and alternates | 100% |
| Communications | Barangays with tested primary and fallback communication channels | 100% |
| SID readiness | Priority hazard-specific anticipatory action protocols approved | All priority hazards identified through technical validation |
| Response | Resource requests logged and tracked during activation | 100% |
| Learning | Major activations and exercises followed by an After-Action Review | 100% |
| Corrective action | High-priority corrective actions completed by agreed deadlines | At least 80%, with reasons recorded for outstanding actions |

These are proposed management targets, not statutory thresholds. They should be adjusted after the City establishes realistic baselines, resources and implementation capacity.
