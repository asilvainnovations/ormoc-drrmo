# ANNEX 2-N \| SOP HR-08 – PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT, RECORDS AND CORRECTIVE ACTION

**Purpose**: Align individual performance with CDRRMO outputs and use evidence to improve capability and institutional processes.

**Owner**: CDRRMO Head and supervisors for operational performance; HRMO for official SPMS and personnel record administration.

**Procedure**:

1\. Cascade approved office targets into individual outputs consistent with official SPMS.

2\. Agree measurable deliverables, indicators, timelines and evidence with each staff member.

3\. Conduct periodic coaching and retain appropriate work evidence.

4\. Assess results fairly against approved standards and consider workload, resources, dependencies and system constraints.

5\. Identify whether the gap is individual, supervisory, procedural, resource-related or inter-agency.

6\. Agree support and corrective action; refer possible misconduct or administrative matters through HRMO and due process.

7\. Verify completion and effectiveness; recognize good performance under approved policies.

8\. Aggregate systemic issues for workforce planning, risk management and MOO revision.

Records: approved performance commitments; coaching records; evidence; improvement plan; CAPA; review and acknowledgement.

Control: this SOP does not create a separate disciplinary process or override CSC rules, due process or the City's SPMS.
