# ANNEX 4-C \| HAZARD-SPECIFIC WARNING AND ACTIVATION MATRIX REGISTER

The City matrix is kept as a controlled register, one entry per hazard and scenario. Table 4.43 is the entry template. Table 4.44 summarises the regional reference triggers; they are not City thresholds until adopted.

**Table 4.43**

*Matrix Register Entry*

| **Field**                        | **Content**                                                                               |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Hazard and scenario**          | Hazard, scenario ID and contingency plan (with version)                                   |
| **Authoritative source product** | Agency, product name and the exact category or wording that triggers the entry            |
| **Adopted by**                   | EOC EO or contingency plan, with number and date; "regional reference only" until adopted |
| **Alert status and actions**     | White, Blue or Red; actions with lead office for each                                     |
| **Decision authority**           | Official or body who decides each action                                                  |
| **Target groups and channels**   | Barangays, facilities and groups; primary and backup channels; accessible formats         |
| **Acknowledgement**              | Who must confirm receipt and by when                                                      |
| **Review**                       | Last review date, exercise and corrective actions                                         |

**Table 4.44**

*Regional Conditions and Triggers for Raising the Alert Status (Summary)*

| **Hazard**                                                                    | **White**                                                                                                      | **Blue**                                                                                         | **Red**                                                                                                              |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Tropical cyclone (flooding, storm surge, severe wind)                         | Weather advisories from PAGASA                                                                                 | PDRA result; Severe Weather Bulletin; tropical depression expected to enter the PAR (TCWS No. 1) | Severe Weather Bulletin; within the PAR; tropical storm (TCWS No. 2) or stronger                                     |
| Other weather disturbances (monsoon, ITCZ, LPA, tail-end of a frontal system) | Yellow rainfall advisory: 7.5–15 mm observed in 1 hour and expected to continue for 2 hours; flooding possible | Orange rainfall advisory: 15–30 mm in 1 hour, expected to continue; flooding threatening         | Red rainfall advisory: more than 30 mm in 1 hour, expected to continue; serious flooding expected in low-lying areas |
| El Niño and La Niña                                                           | Watch alert; climate outlook                                                                                   | Needs mobilisation of national resources                                                         | Needs extensive mobilisation of resources                                                                            |
| Earthquake                                                                    | No damage reported; no tsunami threat                                                                          | Intensity VI expected or reported; minor sea-level disturbance                                   | Intensity VII or above; tsunami warning                                                                              |
| Tsunami                                                                       | No tsunami threat; sea-level monitoring                                                                        | Minor sea-level disturbance: waves under 1 m above expected tides                                | Destructive tsunami: life-threatening waves over 1 m above expected tides; immediate response                        |

*Note.* Summarised from Table 2, Conditions and Triggers in Raising the Alert Status, of the DILG VIII Disaster Response Reporting Template and SOP (RDRRMC VIII Resolution No. 12-006, s. 2023). Either one listed condition or the result of the PDRA or NDRRMC meetings applies. The table also covers volcanoes, tornadoes, conflict, fire, maritime and aviation incidents, CBRNE, epidemics and special events; consult the source for those. Rainfall categories are PAGASA products; the City does not set its own rainfall numbers.
