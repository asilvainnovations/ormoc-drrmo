# Annex 7-D. Post-Declaration (45-day) Report Outline

Basis: RA 12287 IRR Rule VI Sec. 1. The required content (declaration status, AA fund utilisation, estimated economic and social costs minimised) is followed; the remaining sections are proposed to make the report usable for learning. The outline will be revised when the NDRRMC issues the AA monitoring and evaluation framework.

**Table 7.42**

*Contents of the 45-day Report*

| **No.** | **Section**                         | **Content**                                                                                                                     | **Basis**                     |
|---------|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 1       | Declaration status                  | Hazard; area; PDRA; CDRRMC recommendation; RDRRMC VIII action; Mayor's EO; dates of declaration and lifting; ground for lifting | Rule VI Sec. 1                |
| 2       | Anticipatory actions                | Measures carried out by lead office and barangay; people and assets covered (aggregate)                                         | Proposed                      |
| 3       | AA fund utilisation                 | Spending by source and measure; tranche compliance; unused funds transferred to the STF                                         | Rule VI Sec. 1; Rule V Sec. 3 |

| 5       | Lessons                             | Summary of the AAR findings and actions                                                                                         | Proposed                      |
