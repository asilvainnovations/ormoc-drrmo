How to use: zoom and pan freely. Click to drop a site pin, the demo flood footprint recenters on it. Use the Layers control (top-right) to toggle Metro Manila flood hazard overlays from UP NOAH's flood hazard archive (Phil-LiDAR flood hazard maps served via the UP flood-modeling GeoServer). These overlays represent modeled flood hazard extents under 5-, 25-, and 100-year rainfall return period scenarios at 10 m resolution. They are scenario maps, not real-time flooding. Replace the demo footprint with your basin floodplain polygons when you have them, and validate overlays against local gauges, high-water marks, or post-event survey maps.
