MODE User Guide

For CDRRMO and EOC staff of the City of Ormoc · Plain-language guide · MODE v1.22.0 · 17 Nov 2026

Start here

MODE means Manual of Operations for the DRRMO and EOC. It is a website and phone app that keeps our Manual of Operations in one place, helps us follow its steps, and records what we do during an emergency.

You do not need to be good with computers. If you can use Facebook or send a text message, you can use MODE. This manual shows every screen in the order you will meet it, and tells you which button to press.

Five rules to remember

  1. MODE does not issue warnings. For weather and earthquake warnings, use PAGASA and PHIVOLCS. MODE only gives you the links.
  2. Drafts are not official. Anything marked Draft is still being written. The approved Manual of Operations and EOC Manual remain the legal documents.
  3. Never type the names of affected persons, evacuees, patients or informants. Type counts only (for example, 12 families, 48 persons). This follows the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).
  4. It works without internet. What you type is saved on your device and sent later. A few big decisions (like changing the EOC alert level) need a connection.
  5. The logbook cannot be erased. If you make a mistake, write a correction. The first entry stays on record.
?
Tip Hold your finger (or hover your mouse) over any button, tab or box. A small note appears that says what it does and who may use it.

Part 1. Get in

1.1 Open MODE

Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari) and go to the MODE address given by the CDRRMO. You will see the MODE login page with the City seals and the words Prepare Before · Respond Together · Recover Stronger · Build Safer Communities.

1.2 Make a new account (first time only)

Who: any City staff member. Any email address works, including a personal one.

  1. On the login page, tap New account (the tab on the right).
  2. Fill in the boxes:
    • Full name, for example Maria Santos.
    • Position and office (optional), for example LDRRMO III, CDRRMO Operations.
    • Email.
    • Password: at least 10 characters, with letters and a number.
    • Password again: type the same password.
  3. Tap Create account.
  4. Open your email. Tap the confirmation link MODE sent you.
!
If stuck: no email? Check your spam folder. Back on the login page, tap Have an account, type your email, and tap Send the confirmation link again.

1.3 Wait for approval

After you confirm your email, MODE shows Waiting for approval. This is normal. Your account sees no City records until the system administrator gives you a role (see Part 14).

  • You can fix your name or position under Your details, then Save details.
  • Tap Check again to see if you were approved.
  • Tell your administrator you registered. They approve you in Settings → Users and roles.

1.4 Sign in (every other time)

On the login page, stay on Have an account. You have two ways:

WayWhat to do
With a passwordType your Email and Password, then tap the sign-in button.
Without a passwordType your Email, leave Password empty, tap Email me a sign-in link. Open the email on the same device and tap the link.

1.5 Forgot your password

Tap Forgot your password?, type your email, tap Email me a reset link, open the email and choose a new password.

1.6 Put MODE on your phone

MODE is a progressive web app. In simple words: it can sit on your home screen like a normal app and still open without signal. If your browser offers Install or Add to Home screen, accept it. Do this once while you have a good connection, and sign in at least once so your data is saved on the phone.

1.7 Sign out

Where: Settings (bottom of the left menu) → Sign out and clear this device.

This erases MODE’s copy of the data on that device. If you still have entries that have not been sent, MODE warns you first. Connect and tap Sync now (Part 13).

i
Shared phones: always sign out when you hand a phone to someone else.

Part 5. EOC (Emergency Operations Center)

Where: EOC in the menu. The EOC has many tabs under the title: Board · Logbook · SitReps · Requests · Resources · Barangays · Barangay liaison · Risk · Plans and clusters · Roster · Map · Anticipatory action.

5.1 The Board: the EOC at a glance

The Board shows the current situation in blocks. Every block has a link to its full screen.

  • Alert level card (big, top): for example White level — Normal operations, with the Change level button.
  • Logbook: type a quick note and tap Log. All entries opens the full logbook.
  • Barangays: how many barangays reported a condition in the last 24 hours, and how many families and persons are evacuated.
  • Open resource requests, Evacuation centres (ECHO), Latest SitRep, Priority barangays (DDRiVE), EOC capability.

5.2 Change the EOC alert level

Who: EOC Manager, CDRRMO Head, system administrator. Needs a connection.

LevelMeaning in MODE
WhiteNormal operations
BluePartial activation: at least half of resources on standby
RedFull activation: all resources available
  1. EOC → Board → tap Change level.
  2. Choose the new level. The level you are on now is greyed out and says Current level.
  3. Type the Reason. This is required. It goes into the logbook and shows in the top bar.
  4. Optional: choose the Hazard, and type the Authority or memo reference.
  5. Tap the confirm button at the bottom of the panel. The change is logged with your name, the time and the reason.

5.3 Logbook: the official diary

Write an entry: Tap New entry. Pick a Category (General, Decision, Warning, Request, Resource, Report). Type the Entry. Tap save.

Fix a mistake: Tap the wrong entry. In Correct an entry, type the Corrected entry. Save. A new entry is added and linked to the old one. The old one stays visible with a line through it.

i
You cannot edit or delete entries, and you cannot correct a Level change entry.

5.4 Barangay status reports

  1. Tap the barangay.
  2. Choose the Condition: Normal, Monitoring, Affected, Isolated or Evacuating.
  3. If evacuating, type Families evacuated and Persons evacuated. Otherwise leave them blank.
  4. Choose Received by: Radio, SMS, Phone call, This app or In person.
  5. Add Remarks if needed. No names.
  6. Tap Record report.
!
Blank is not zero. If the barangay did not give a number, leave the box empty. MODE keeps it as not reported. Typing 0 means they reported zero.

5.5 Resource requests

A request moves through five steps: Requested → Approved → Mobilized → Delivered → Demobilized. A request can also be Rejected.

  1. Tap New request.
  2. Fill in Requested by, Item, Quantity, Unit, and the priority (Life safety, Urgent or Routine).
  3. Save. It appears under Open.

5.6 Resource inventory

A list of what the EOC can use: vehicles, heavy equipment, rescue, medical, relief goods, communications, power and others. Find things with Find a resource, Kind, or Barangay or place.

5.7 SitReps (situation reports)

A SitRep is the official report sent to OCD VIII and the Province. MODE follows the DILG VIII harmonized template. It moves Draft → Submitted → Approved.

  1. Tap New SitRep. Do this once for each reporting period during an activation.
  2. Set the Reporting period: From and To.
  3. Under DILG VIII harmonized fields, choose the Report type and fill in the fields.
  4. Under Situation, write the overview, actions taken and needs.
  5. Under Figures, tap Fill from barangay reports and Add ECHO camp figures.
  6. Tap Save. When ready, tap Submit. The SitRep gets its number.
  7. The EOC Manager, CDRRMO Head or administrator taps Approve. An approved SitRep is locked.

5.8 Barangay liaison desk

Shows, by barangay cluster, which barangays have reported and which are silent. Tap a cluster to see its barangays. Silent barangays are listed first, ranked by DDRiVE priority. Counts only.

5.9 Risk (DDRiVE)

A City risk picture and a Barangay profile for each barangay. Filter by hazard type. Aggregate data only. Decision support only — never an evacuation trigger.

5.10 Plans and clusters

This activation shows which plan, response clusters and EOC modules are switched on. Each is marked Lead, Activated, On standby or Stood down.

5.11 Roster

Every EOC position needs a Primary and two alternates so the EOC can run for 72 hours. The Depth meter shows how full the roster is.

Operations Section — roster depth5 / 6 filled

5.12 Map (Geo-COP)

The common operating picture: City hazard map library, reference points (EOC, alternate EOC, staging areas, evacuation hubs), and layers (KML, KMZ, GeoJSON).

5.13 Anticipatory action (acting before a disaster)

This follows RA 12287 (Imminent Disaster Act) and its rules.

Step A. Assess: New PDRA

  1. Tap New PDRA (Pre-Disaster Risk Assessment).
  2. Forecast: choose the Hazard; type the Forecast source; set Forecast issued and Expected impact.
  3. Projected impact: choose Severe, Moderate, Minor or Uncertain; describe cascading hazards; tick the barangays.
  4. Decision: choose one — Monitor, City early action, or Request a State of Imminent Disaster.

Step B. Follow the declaration, in order

  1. Pre-disaster risk assessment.
  2. Request to RDRRMC VIII. Tap Record request sent. This starts the 24-hour reply clock.
  3. RDRRMC recommendation. Record Recommended or Not recommended.
  4. Mayor’s Executive Order. Type the Executive Order number and record it.
  5. Anticipatory actions and funds. Tap to load the action checklist. The CDRRMO Head records releases under Funds released.
  6. Lifting. Tap Record lifting. This starts the 45-day clock.
  7. Report to OCD. Tap Record report sent within 45 days.

6.1 Start a protocol

  1. Tap the protocol in the list. A panel opens with the steps, the process owner, and the forms.
  2. Check Procedure source. It links to the Manual section in the Library. RACI for (code) shows who does what.
  3. If this is an exercise, tick This is a drill.
  4. Tap Start protocol (or Start drill).

6.2 Work through the steps

  1. Do the action in real life.
  2. Fill in the step’s form if it has one. Add a Note.
  3. Close the step with the done button. MODE records who closed it and when.
  4. When every critical step is closed, tap Complete.

6.3 Drills count

A run started as a drill is recorded as a drill. This shows that a procedure has been tested, which helps the readiness assessment.

7.2 Assessment cycles

A cycle is one round of assessment, for example 2026 Baseline (IAT v1.7). Tap a cycle to open it.

7.3 Read a cycle

At the top: a progress bar and buttons for Validation workshop, Action plan, EOC capability checklist, Export CSV.

Institutional Maturity Index2.4 / 4.0 · Developing

Results shows four scores: the Institutional Maturity Index (A+B), Part A (CDRRMO), Part B (EOC), and Part C (Interface Index). Each has a number out of 4.0, a word such as Developing or Initial, and Mandatory met.

7.4 Score one indicator

  1. Open the indicator.
  2. Under Evidence, tap Add evidence. Choose the Type of evidence and What it shows, give it a Title, the Document date, and the Source office or position. Never type an informant’s name.
  3. Under Score, choose the Maturity level.
  4. Add Gap or remarks and Recommended action.
  5. Tap Save. Then send the score to a validator.
  6. A validator confirms it. If the validator changes the score, a Reason for the change is required.

7.5 Hazard maps

Official hazard sheets drawn over the barangays. Use the buttons above the map: Identify, Distance, Area, My location, Clear, Export PNG, Full screen, +/−.

The sheets are reference pictures: they do not forecast and do not trigger anything.

Part 9. Vault (documents)

Where: Vault. Three tabs: Documents, Document Review, References.

The Vault is one place for laws, guidelines, City issuances, plans and records.

9.1 Find and open a document

  1. Type in the search box (title, number, issuer or indicator), or use All types.
  2. Tap a document. Tap Open file to read the attached copy. The link is private and works for five minutes.
  3. Evidence for lists the indicators this document supports.

9.2 Add a document

  1. Tap Add document.
  2. Fill in the details and do one of: upload a file, add a link, or say where the paper original is kept.
  3. Choose the indicators it supports, and save.

9.3 Replace old versions

Documents are never deleted. Use Mark as superseded when a newer version exists.

9.5 References tab

A catalogue of the laws and guidelines cited in MODE, each explained in plain language. Tapping a citation such as RA 10121 Sec. 21 anywhere in MODE opens a short note.

13.2 What works without internet

Works offline: reading anything already loaded, Search, logbook entries, barangay reports, new resource requests, SitRep drafts, running protocols, proposing roster designations, the ICS, PSCP, KALASAG and ECHO simulator tools.

Needs a connection: changing the EOC alert level, approving anything, declaration steps and fund releases, activating a plan, saving resource edits, validating scores, and managing accounts.

13.3 The Sync and offline data page

Where: Settings → Sync and offline data, or tap the sync words.

  • Sync now sends waiting changes and fetches the latest data. Retry now tries waiting changes again.
  • Refused entries: the server said no, with its reason in plain words. Copy keeps what you typed so you can enter it again. Dismiss removes the message.
  • Conflicts: someone else changed the same record. Apply my change on top keeps the other person’s version and adds your changed fields. Keep theirs drops yours.
  • Files that could not upload: Retry upload, Save a copy to your device, or Discard (it asks you to confirm first).

Part 18. One-page quick card

Print this page and keep it at your desk.

  1. Check the top bar first: EOC level and Synced.
  2. Warnings come from PAGASA and PHIVOLCS, not from MODE.
  3. Log it: EOC → Logbook → New entry. Never erase, only correct.
  4. Barangay report: EOC → Barangays → tap barangay → Record report. Blank means not reported.
  5. Need something: EOC → Requests → New request.
  6. Report up: EOC → SitReps → New SitRep → Submit.
  7. Follow a procedure: Protocols → tap one → Start protocol (use Start drill for practice).
  8. No names of evacuees, patients or informants. Ever.
  9. Offline is fine. Level changes and approvals need signal.
  10. Lost? Press Ctrl+K or tap Search. Ask your administrator for help with roles and accounts.
