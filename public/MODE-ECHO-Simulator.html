Hazard and displacement assumptions

Scenario guardrails

Simulator inputs are not evacuation triggers. Use official PAGASA / PHIVOLCS / MGB information, validated local thresholds and authorized City instructions.
  • Do not infer that an unselected barangay is safe.
  • Schools are a last resort under RA 10821 where no other suitable structure is available.
  • Include children, women, older persons, PWDs and Indigenous Peoples in aggregate planning.
  • Account for host communities and people sheltering outside formal centres.
  • Missing counts remain “not reported”, not zero.

Legal and humanitarian reference

Standards & References

The simulator translates selected requirements into prompts and indicators; it does not replace official texts.

Philippine legal framework

RA 10121 — Philippine DRRM Act; local authority, coordination and response.

RA 10821 — Children’s Emergency Relief and Protection Act; child protection, child-friendly spaces and child-specific relief; schools only as last resort where no other suitable structure is available.

RA 9710 — Magna Carta of Women; gender-responsive services, privacy, safety and participation.

RA 7277, as amended — Magna Carta for Persons with Disability; accessibility and equal access.

RA 9994 — Expanded Senior Citizens Act; age-responsive access and appropriate facilities.

RA 8371 — Indigenous Peoples’ Rights Act; respect for culture, participation and applicable rights.

RA 11106 — Filipino Sign Language Act; accessible communication and interpretation.

RA 12076 — Ligtas Pinoy Centers Act (2024); permanent, disaster-resilient evacuation centres and minimum facility standards.

Operational standards

DSWD-DILG-DOH-DepEd JMC 02 s. 2021 — Camp Management Committee and Team, DAFAc-based profiling, information boards, camp layout, WASH, health, CFS, WFS, protection, reporting, monitoring and closure.

Sphere Handbook 2018 — Humanitarian Charter, Protection Principles, Core Humanitarian Standard, WASH, food security and nutrition, shelter and settlement, health.

IOM CCCM — Set-up / improvement, care and maintenance, closure; coordination, information management, participation, complaints and feedback, infrastructure maintenance and protection mainstreaming.

JMC indicators, Sphere benchmarks and RA 12076 requirements are not interchangeable. Confirm the applicable and stricter requirement with technical offices. Legal interpretation and current implementing rules require City Legal / OCD validation.
